# Savenae Smooth Reset Pads: product and brand teardown and implied ICP (Bulgaria, as of 6 Oct 2026)

Method note: All savenae.com data was pulled on 2026-10-06 with direct HTTP requests: the product HTML, the Shopify JSON endpoints (`/products/<handle>.json`, `/products.json`, `/meta.json`), the policy pages, the sitemap and the blog Atom feed. The 10 product images were downloaded and read visually. Firecrawl was used for the Meta Ad Library, Linktree and web/Alibaba searches. Firecrawl does not support Instagram or TikTok. No orders were placed and no paid connectors were used. The Firecrawl account reported low credits near the end of the session.

## 1. What the product is: name, format, formula, claims, usage, body areas, price/offers, guarantee, shipping, payment, language

### Takeaway
"SAVENAE Ексфолиращи тампони при враснали косъмчета" (internal English name **Smooth Reset Pads**) is a jar of **50 thin, round, textured, pre-soaked exfoliating wipe-on pads**. The label shows "100 ml" and **"AHA + BHA"**, but **no INCI list, acid names or concentrations appear anywhere on the site**. The price is **€18.90 (compare-at €22.90)**, with **free shipping, cash on delivery, a 30-day money-back guarantee, "ships from Bulgaria"**, and the product is sold as a **pre-order**. Everything is in Bulgarian, priced in EUR and shipped to Bulgaria only. The copy carefully limits use to **legs, underarms and the *outer* bikini line**, after **shaving or waxing**.

### Inferences
- "Остави формулата да попие" together with "100 ml" in a 50-pad jar means the pads are pre-soaked in a leave-on acid solution, the same format as First Aid Beauty and Bushbalm pads. They are not dry textured discs.
- The copy reads as deliberately compliance-aware. It avoids treatment claims, uses "подпомага", carries disclaimers, labels the advertorial "Рекламен материал", cites NHS and narrows the area to the "outer" bikini line. It looks written to pass Meta ad review and EU Regulation 655/2013 claim criteria. The trade-off is that the copy promises little: no speed, no pigmentation and no intimate-zone claims.
- Deferring frequency and directions to "the label" while showing no label text online suggests the final formula and label may not be fixed yet. That fits the pre-order status (see section 2).
- The English-only pack and the conflicting images (intimate zone and face/neck in images, "outer bikini line only, not face/neck" in text) look like a fast launch with stock or AI-generated imagery that was not fully aligned with the copy.

### Gaps
- No INCI, acid types, concentrations or pH could be found. The physical label could not be inspected without ordering, which was not allowed.
- Whether the shipped product will carry a Bulgarian-language label, and whether a CPNP notification exists, could not be verified.
- The checkout was not opened, so COD as an actual checkout option (rather than a policy statement) and any COD fee were not verified.

## 6. Derived: the implied ICP, and how it fits or misfits the Bulgarian market

### Takeaway
The page speaks to a **Bulgarian woman, roughly 20–35 (inferred), who shaves her legs at home, plus underarms and bikini line, and sometimes uses кола маска or an epilator**. Her problem is **mild, cosmetic and frequent: red dots, rough "strawberry-leg" texture and occasional ingrown hairs the morning after shaving**. She wants a **fast, clean, 30-second after-shower step** and a calm "natural skin" aesthetic, not a miracle. The pitch is a habit product for the legs, not a fix for the painful bikini-zone problem. This creates a **mismatch with the dominant Bulgarian pain**: Bulgarian sufferers talk mostly about the bikini/intimate zone ("триъгълник", "там долу", "топче до слабините"), painful bumps, scars and dark spots, and long histories of failed remedies before laser. Savenae deliberately restricts itself to the "outer" bikini line and makes no pigmentation or results claims.

### Inferences: the implied ICP
- **Who**: a Bulgarian-speaking woman living in Bulgaria, likely about 20–35, style-conscious and drawn to calm, "clean girl", spa-bathroom aesthetics. She is probably a frequent home shaver; Instagram and TikTok exposure to "strawberry legs" content is plausible.
- **Hair removal**: mainly razor shaving of the legs, then underarms and outer bikini line. Some at-home or salon кола маска or epilator use. She is **not** a laser user or laser-intender, and the page never addresses that path.
- **Severity**: mild to moderate cosmetic irritation (red dots, rough texture, occasional ingrown hairs). She does **not** have painful inflamed bumps, cysts, scarring or hyperpigmentation, none of which the page mentions.
- **Motivation**: a maintenance habit ("след всяко бръснене… а не само преди лятото") and a "по-поддържан" look, more than acute shame or a pre-beach emergency.
- **Buying behaviour**: a cautious first-time buyer of an unknown brand who wants COD, free delivery to an office or address, a refund guarantee and local dispatch. She is price-aware (€18.90, SAVENAE10).

### Inferences: mismatch with the Bulgarian market
1. **Zone mismatch.** Bulgarian demand and competitor messaging centre on the bikini/intimate zone. Savenae leads with legs and restricts itself to the "outer" bikini line, while one image says "интимна зона". The page under-serves the highest-pain segment and also sends mixed signals.
2. **Severity and outcome mismatch.** Bulgarians describe scars, dark spots and painful bumps, and competitors promise to "изсветли петната". Savenae offers only "по-гладко усещане". That is safer for compliance but weaker for a solution-aware, burned-before buyer who has "tried everything".
3. **Gender exclusion.** Savenae is women-only. LUMI and PFB Vanish openly include men (beard, neck, chest), so Savenae leaves out male shavers, and its images even show a jaw/neck it tells buyers not to use it on.
4. **Trust deficit in a COD market.** Zero reviews, a pre-order with unconfirmed dispatch, a Gmail address, no legal entity or EIK (see ЗЕТ чл. 4) and English-only packaging shots all weigh against competitors' heavy social proof. Pre-order plus COD also raises the risk of refused parcels.
5. **Transparency gap.** "AHA + BHA" with no INCI or percentages, and directions deferred to an unseen label. Ingredient-savvy buyers comparing against FAB or PFB Vanish get nothing to compare.
6. **Format and price fit is good.** The pad format is a white space in Bulgaria: no active BG pad ads, and no in-stock branded pads at major retailers. The price undercuts the DTC roll-ons, and the Bulgarian language, "враснали" spelling, COD and free shipping match local buying habits.

### Gaps
- Actual buyer demographics are unknown: no reviews, no ads and no audience-transparency data.
- No Bulgarian search-volume data was available to size the "legs/strawberry legs" segment against the "bikini/intimate" segment. Google Trends was refused in earlier research.
- It is unknown whether Savenae will add men, intimate-zone or pigmentation messaging once ads start.
