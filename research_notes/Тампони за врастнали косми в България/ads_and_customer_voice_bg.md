# Ingrown-hair products for the bikini / intimate area in Bulgaria: advertising landscape and voice of the customer (research date: 6 Oct 2026)

How the research was done: the Meta Ad Library was scraped with Firecrawl on 2026-10-06 (country=BG, active ads, keyword search). Meta returned results sorted by total impressions, highest first (the redirected URL contains `sort_data[mode]=total_impressions`), so ads near the top had more reach. The "~N results" figures are Meta's own estimates and count every ad version, including duplicates. Landing-page prices came from each Shopify store's public `/products/<handle>.js` endpoint (fetched with curl) and from page HTML. Google Trends returned HTTP 429 every time (see Gaps under question 5).

## 1. Meta Ad Library (Bulgaria): who advertises ingrown-hair products, with what hooks, offers and landing pages

### Takeaway
Two BG Shopify DTC brands advertise an at-home ingrown-hair product directly: **Deroli Cosmetics** (derolibg.com, ~14 active ads) and **LUMI** (lumibg.com, 5 active ads). Both sell a near-identical BHA/AHA/PHA + niacinamide **roll-on serum** (not pads) at about €20, with a "7 days" promise, a bikini-zone focus and heavy discount framing. The rest of the paid space belongs to **laser clinics** (the largest group by volume), razors and trimmers, scrubs and exfoliating mitts, hair-growth-inhibitor oils and serums, and IPL devices. I found **no active Meta ad in BG for pre-soaked ingrown-hair pads**, which suggests the pad format is still open on paid social.

### Inferences
- The at-home "ingrown hair" niche on BG Meta is a two-player DTC fight, Deroli and LUMI. Both sell the same roll-on formula type (Deroli's and LUMI's listed ingredients match almost exactly; see question 3), so the products are likely white-label or private-label. Deroli refreshed its creatives on 1 Oct 2026 and LUMI relaunched 28 Sep–4 Oct 2026. Both are investing in Q4, so the category is not only a summer play.
- No pad or "тампони/кърпички" ingrown-hair product appears in any query. A pre-soaked pad would be differentiated by format ("едно избърсване" versus a roll-on) even though the actives are similar.
- The most common creative frames are: (1) "everything failed: painful, expensive salon/laser, creams don't work"; (2) "stop hiding: beach and intimate moments"; (3) a mechanism story about pores clogged with dead cells; (4) a named city-tagged testimonial ("Мария К. от Пловдив"); (5) a 7-day result promise plus dark-spot lightening as a second benefit. Dark spots and pigmentation ("тъмни петна", "белези") are used as a second benefit in every DTC ad.
- Laser clinics teach the market that ingrown hairs are a hair-removal side effect and position laser as the cure. A pad brand can position itself as the cheap, at-home alternative for people not ready to pay for laser, an objection that appears in the forums (question 4).
- The misspelling "враснали" returned about as many ads as "врастнали". Copy and keyword targeting should cover both spellings plus "подкожни косми/косъмчета" and "обърнати косъмчета".

### Gaps
- The Ad Library HTML does not expose spend, reach or creative video content (video ads showed "Sorry, we're having trouble playing this video"), so the hooks above are primary text and link cards only.
- Ads were checked on Facebook/Instagram only. TikTok's Ad Library (Commercial Content Library) and the Google Ads Transparency Center were not scraped, so TikTok and Google paid activity is unverified.
- Inactive and historical ads were not reviewed, so I cannot tell how long earlier ingrown-hair advertisers ran or whether pad sellers tested and stopped.

## 3. Bulgarian landing pages, DTC shops and retail: headlines, prices, bundles, guarantees, claims

## 4. Voice of the customer: Bulgarian words, pain points, failed solutions, objections

### Takeaway
Bulgarians call the problem **"подкожни косми/косъмчета"** as much as "врастнали/враснали косми". The richest organic forum VOC is **old (2010–2020)**; the recent (2024–2026) VOC found is seller-curated reviews on DTC pages.

## 6. Regulatory and claim limits for advertising this category in BG/EU and on Meta (brief)

### Takeaway
Ingrown-hair pads are sold as **cosmetics**, so claims must meet **Regulation (EU) No 655/2013's common criteria** (truthful, evidence-backed, honest and fair). Meta's **Health & Wellness policy** bans negative self-perception and attacks on body parts or hygiene and restricts before/after imagery. Several live BG competitor claims look risky on both fronts: "Единственият продукт…", "Премахва на 100 %", "98% от жените…", "клинично изследване с 30 участнички", a named "Д-р Тодоров", and shame-based hooks.

### Cited Findings
- Regulation (EU) No 655/2013 sets "общи критерии за обосноваване използването на претенции, отнасящи се до козметични продукти" (common criteria for justifying cosmetic product claims) — [EUR-Lex (BG)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/BG/ALL/?uri=CELEX:32013R0655); summary: claims "must meet… clear, truthful and not misleading" — [BeLab Services](https://www.belabservices.com/en/post/cosmetic-product-claims-european-union-regulation-requirements)
- The Bulgarian Ministry of Health has published guidance on example cosmetic claims ("Предложения за примерни претенции на козметични продукти") — [mh.government.bg PDF](https://www.mh.government.bg/upload/8983/predlojenia.pdf)
- Meta's Health and Wellness policy restricts ads that contain "statements of inferiority about physical appearance (i.e., terms, descriptions, or questions that are negative and attack an individual's appearance, specific body parts, or hygiene)" — [Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness/); Meta's help page shows before/after skin treatment images as non-compliant examples — [Meta Business Help](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2489235377779939)
- An industry summary of Meta's 2026 rules: idealized-result before/after is barred, and any creative that "implies or attempts to generate negative self-perception" is non-compliant — [AuditSocials](https://www.auditsocials.com/blog/meta-beauty-cosmetic-ads-before-after-photos-body-image-policy-2026); phrases like "don't be embarrassed anymore" are flagged as risky — [Zappush](https://www.zappush.com/blog/why-meta-doesnt-allow-before-and-after-images-in-health-ads)
- Live BG examples carrying claim risk: "Единственият продукт, който премахва врастнали косми…" (LUMI) — [ad](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1072774725622074); "98% от жените виждат видима разлика" and "Дерматологично тествано и одобрено" (Deroli) — [ad](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1029734312805937); "Премахва на 100 % врасналите в кожата косми" (Framar listing for Ingrow Go) — [Framar](https://apteka.framar.bg/30029040/%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%BD-%D0%B4%D0%BE%D0%BA%D1%82%D0%BE%D1%80%D1%81-ingrow-go-%D0%BB%D0%BE%D1%81%D0%B8%D0%BE%D0%BD-120-%D0%BC%D0%BB); "Клинично изследване с 30 участнички за 4 седмици" and "Одобрен и препоръчан от дерматолози… Д-р Тодоров" (LUMI page) — [lumibg.com](https://lumibg.com/products/lumi-ingrown)
- Precedent: explicit external-intimate-zone cosmetic ads ("МИРИЗМА МЕЖДУ КРАКАТА", "Притеснението — в бикините") are currently active in BG — [NONA ad](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1067157216308795)

### Inferences
- Bikini- and intimate-zone ingrown-hair ads are evidently being approved in BG (Deroli, LUMI and NONA are live). The risk lies in specific framings: shame questions such as "Уморена ли си да криеш кожата си?", close-ups of inflamed bumps, side-by-side before/after shots, and absolute claims ("единственият", "100%", "за 7 дни"). These are the elements most likely to cause disapprovals or account restrictions. Safer framing: benefit-led ("гладка, спокойна кожа след бръснене"), mechanism education (exfoliating acids that help prevent ingrown hairs), and testimonials that do not disparage prior appearance.
- Medical-sounding terms ("лечение", "инфекция", "фоликулит", "премахва 100%") may push a cosmetic toward medicinal-product claims under EU and BG rules. Percentage claims, "dermatologically tested" and "clinical study" need a real test dossier behind them.

### Gaps
- No BG-specific enforcement cases (КЗП or the Ministry of Health) on ingrown-hair product claims were found. The Unfair Commercial Practices Directive angle (fake countdown timers and stock badges) was not researched in depth.
- Meta's exact current wording for "Personal Health and Appearance" rules covering intimate areas was not retrieved verbatim beyond the Health & Wellness page excerpt above.
