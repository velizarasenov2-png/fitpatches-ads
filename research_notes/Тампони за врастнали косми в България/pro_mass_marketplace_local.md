# Ingrown-hair pads in Bulgaria: professional/salon, mass-market, marketplace/generic and Bulgarian-local brands

Research date: 6 Oct 2026. Bulgaria has used the euro since 1 Jan 2026. Many Bulgarian shops still show both currencies at the fixed rate 1 EUR = 1.95583 BGN ([Makave Shop](https://shop.makave.eu/bg/body/vrastnali-kosmi/)).

Method caveats (read first):
- **eMAG.bg** sent every scrape and fetch (Firecrawl basic and stealth, WebFetch, curl) to a captcha (HTTP 511), so I could not open eMAG search pages. The eMAG data below comes from search-engine snippets of eMAG pages. Prices and ratings are what the snippet showed and may be a few weeks old. Treat them as indicative.
- **Temu** redirects to a login wall, and **Trendyol BG**, **Skroutz.bg**, 366.bg and baby.bg returned 403. My AliExpress and Amazon.de scrapes came back in a US view (USD, "Delivers to United States"), so those prices are not Bulgarian checkout prices.
- I did not cover premium international brands (Bushbalm, Fur, Tend Skin, Bliss, First Aid Beauty, etc.) in depth. They are mentioned only where they appear in the channels below.

## 1. Professional wax/depilation brands: which have ingrown-hair pads or post-epilation anti-ingrown products in Bulgaria?

### Takeaway
I found **no professional wax brand selling ingrown-hair pads (pre-soaked discs or wipes) through a Bulgarian channel.** Professional brands in Bulgaria sell liquids instead: lotions, sprays, serums, foams, after-wax milks and oils, and scrubs. The products closest to pads are Lycon's Ingrown-X-it Wipes, which exist internationally but are not in Lycon Bulgaria's catalogue, and generic face toner pads on eMAG.

- Bulgarian prices for pro anti-ingrown products run from about €4 (Alveola 100 ml spray on eMAG) to €11 (Ro.ial 500 ml milk) and €16–25 (Perron Rigot serum or foam). This sets a reference band against which a pad product would be compared.

## 2. What Bulgarian waxing and laser salons sell or recommend to clients for ingrown hairs

### Takeaway
Bulgarian laser clinics mostly present **laser hair removal as the cure** for ingrown hairs. Their blog content recommends exfoliation (enzyme powder or light glycolic peel rather than scrubs), hydration and avoiding DIY extraction, and they rarely name a take-home product. Wax shops and studios recommend exfoliation plus after-wax lotion. **No salon content I found recommends pads.**

### Cited Findings
- **Discret Lazer** (rating snippet 5.0, 324 reviews) says you do not need a scrub. Use "ензимен прах или лек гликолов пилинг" (enzyme powder or a light glycolic peel) instead. It calls laser "Най-добрият начин" (the best way) and says 3–4 procedures are needed when there are many ingrowns, with ingrowns "actively coming out" by procedures 4–6. It also advises against depilatory creams and epilators on affected skin. — [discretlazer.com blog](https://discretlazer.com/blog/vrastnali-kosmi-po-tyaloto)
- **Kolamaska.com** (wax shop blog) gives 4 tips: exfoliate before epilation, epilate in the direction of growth, hydrate with "лосион за след епилация" (after-epilation lotion) or thermal water, and use wax because it removes the dead skin layer. — [kolamaska.com blog](https://kolamaska.com/blog?journal_blog_post_id=16)
- **Skin Laser** (Veliko Tarnovo, Естетик партнърс ЕООД) has a blog post on causes and treatment. The search snippet says astringent/drying products "са подходящи след бръснене, понеже стягат и изсушават" (suit post-shave use because they tighten and dry the skin) but require extra care. The page links to laser bikini-zone pricing. — [epilacia-skinlaser.bg](https://epilacia-skinlaser.bg/grizha-za-kozhata/vrastnali-kosmi-prichini-lechenie/)
- **Armada Clinic** discourages home extraction ("Опитите за изваждане в домашни условия могат да доведат до инфекция, възпаление и белези", i.e. home extraction attempts can cause infection, inflammation and scars), mentions "подходящи локални продукти" (suitable topical products) without naming brands, and promotes laser. — [armadaclinic.com](https://armadaclinic.com/vrastnali-kosmi/)
- **Laser Clinic Paris** has a dedicated "Врастнали косми" (ingrown hairs) zone page selling laser epilation as removal of ingrown hairs. — [laserclinicparis.com](https://laserclinicparis.com/zoni-za-epilacia/vrastnali-kosmi/)

- **Bebok.eu** (salon article) uses the **PFB Vanish** depigmenting roll-on serum for ingrown-hair removal and prevention. — [bebok.eu](https://bebok.eu/article/2504-%D0%9D%D0%B5-%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B7%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%BD%D1%8F%D0%B2%D0%B0%D0%B9%D1%82%D0%B5-%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%B6%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B0-%D1%81%D0%B8-%E2%80%93-%D0%BC%D0%B0%D1%85%D0%BD%D0%B5%D1%82%D0%B5-%D0%BB%D0%B5%D1%81%D0%BD%D0%BE-%D0%BF%D0%BE%D0%B4%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%B6%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%82%D0%B5-%D0%BA%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%8A%D0%BC%D1%87%D0%B5%D1%82%D0%B0)

- Salons see ingrown hairs as a reason to **sell more laser sessions**. Take-home retail is weak or generic. There is an opening for a salon-friendly **take-home pad** that clinics could resell or recommend between laser sessions. This is not validated.
- The language salons use, which an ad could mirror: "врастнали/подкожни косми" (ingrown/under-skin hairs), "пъпчици след кола маска" (bumps after wax), "зачервяване" (redness), "белези/петна" (scars/marks), "ексфолиране" (exfoliation), "гликолов пилинг" (glycolic peel), "без чоплене" (no picking).

### Gaps
- I did not find any Bulgarian salon price list showing retail aftercare products for sale at the salon.
- Several salon pages (e.g., Skin Laser) are JavaScript-rendered, so I could not read their full article bodies.

### Inferences
- The Bulgarian DTC "product-store" playbook has already reached the ingrown category in the **roll-on/serum** format. LUMI and DEROLI use identical positioning: a 7-day promise, bikini and intimate focus, review-heavy pages, about 50% "compare-at" anchors, and 2-unit gift bundles. Neither offers **pads**. A pad product would differentiate on format (pre-dosed, single-use, hygienic, travel and gym friendly) while competing in the same €20/unit, €40/2-unit price band.
- LUMI's "7356 клиента" (7,356 customers) counter and DEROLI's "100+ ревюта" (100+ reviews) are unverified marketing claims. On-page review counts (LUMI 34) are much smaller.
- Given Bulgarian DTC norms, COD at checkout is likely but unverified for both brands.
