# Case Studies & Teardowns of Weight-Loss / Metabolic Advertorials and Pre-Sell Funnels (2022–Oct 2026)

Scope note: this file covers named, concrete examples and what they teach. Verification labels used throughout:
- **[VERIFIED-LIVE]**: I fetched the live page myself (Oct 3, 2026) and saw the element.
- **[REPORTED]**: reported by a third-party journalist/analyst/tool with a named source; not independently checked.
- **[CLAIMED]**: self-reported by a brand, affiliate, network or course seller (marketing material); treat as unaudited.
- Several of the European COD case studies with hard numbers are from 2019–2020, i.e. older than the 2022–2026 window; they are included because they are the only public COD weight-loss cases with spend/revenue numbers I found, and are flagged as such.

## 1. Which weight-loss / metabolic brands are known for scaling with advertorials, pre-landers, or quiz pre-sells (2022–2026)?

### Takeaway
Publicly documented teardowns with real numbers are scarce; the best-documented weight-loss/metabolic examples are SkinnyFit (quiz pre-sell), Kind Patches (berberine/"GLP-1" patch on TikTok Shop), Lemme GLP-1 Daily (creator/affiliate-led), Sculptique (testimonial-led Meta scale), Mitolyn (ClickBank VSL) and the European COD offers (Ideal Slim, Keto Garcinia, Dietonus, Keto Diet) run through Dr.Cash/LeadBit/Everad-style "prelanding + landing" funnels. Brand-owned DTC advertorials (Arrae, Pendulum, Bioma, Nooro) are confirmed to exist but their copy and performance are not published in any teardown I could reach.

### Cited Findings

**Case 1 — SkinnyFit "GLP-1 Natural Slimming Probiotic" (US, Meta → quiz → offer)** [REPORTED by Funnel of the Week, which buys the products as a customer; date not shown on page]
- Traffic: Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ads; ad messaging emphasises "Ozempic-like results" at a fraction of the cost and a "try before you buy" angle with "$5 for shipping" front-end — [Funnel of the Week: 8 Figure Quiz Funnel Breakdown](https://blog.funneloftheweek.com/p/8-figure-quiz-funnel-breakdown)
- Format: quiz-to-ecommerce, 12 questions; ad talks weight loss/Ozempic, but quiz widens to skin, hair, nails, joints, digestion, energy ("context shift"), includes "future-pacing" breather questions and age-specific (female) framing — [Funnel of the Week](https://blog.funneloftheweek.com/p/8-figure-quiz-funnel-breakdown)
- Proof: a "98 lbs" before/after testimonial shown on the "results loading" screen after the quiz — [Funnel of the Week](https://blog.funneloftheweek.com/p/8-figure-quiz-funnel-breakdown)
- Offer/AOV: "$5 front end, $268 cart value" achieved through 3 pre-purchase order bumps (incl. a continuity option) plus 5 upsells — [Funnel of the Week](https://blog.funneloftheweek.com/p/8-figure-quiz-funnel-breakdown)
- Performance: described as "8-figure"; FOTW says it gets ~30% more traffic than a competitor benchmarked at $60M/year (MyObvi). These are traffic-estimate comparisons, not audited revenue [CLAIMED/ESTIMATE] — [Funnel of the Week](https://blog.funneloftheweek.com/p/8-figure-quiz-funnel-breakdown)
- Elsewhere FOTW describes a weight-loss ad driving to a page where ~90% of the content is about something other than weight loss (skin, hair etc.) while keeping the weight-loss thread — [Funnel of the Week search summary / FOTW site](https://get.funneloftheweek.com/)

**Case 2 — "Double advertorial" (Fixd; not weight loss, but the most-cited numeric pre-sell case)** [CLAIMED by John Belcher, AdSkills co-founder, via Funnel of the Week]
- Traffic: YouTube. Evolution: Mini-VSL → product page; then Mini-VSL → Advertorial 1 → product page (at $3–5k/day); then Mini-VSL → Advertorial 1 (problem/solution/offer) → Advertorial 2 (concentrated social proof) → product page — [Funnel of the Week: when 2 advertorials are better than 1](https://blog.funneloftheweek.com/p/2-advertorials-better-1)
- Reported numbers: ~30% CTR from advertorial 1, ~80% CTR from advertorial 2 to product page; CPA cut "by over 50%", enabling scale to "$20k/day+"; original bottleneck was cart-to-purchase rate well below a 30% benchmark — [Funnel of the Week](https://blog.funneloftheweek.com/p/2-advertorials-better-1)

- Funnel: Advertorial → Product page → Checkout → Upsell → Thank-you; personalised timelines ("First visible improvements: 5–7 days", "Full elimination: estimated by [date]") instead of fake countdowns; bundles priced per unit so bigger bundles show bigger per-unit savings; continuity offers [REPORTED via template sellers and FOTW] — [Funnel of the Week: 3 unconventional ways to boost AOV](https://blog.funneloftheweek.com/p/3-unconventional-ways-to-boost-your-aov); [Scribd: Nooro advertorial structure](https://www.scribd.com/document/879328616/Nooro-Ecommerce-Advertorial-1)

**Cases 12–15 — European COD nutra (see Q5 for detail)**: Ideal Slim (IT, Dr.Cash), Keto Garcinia (RO), Dietonus (ES), Keto Diet (CZ, LeadBit), Harmonica (TR, doctor-interview prelander).

### Inferences
- In the US DTC world, the strongest public evidence points to **multi-step pre-sell** (quiz or two advertorials) beating ad → PDP for cold weight-loss traffic, with the money made on AOV stacking (bumps, bundles, upsells) rather than the front-end price.
- The 2024–2026 "GLP-1" naming wave (Lemme, SkinnyFit, Pendulum, Kind Patches) delivered attention but also class actions, media exposés and forced renames; Kind Patches' rename to "Berberine (GLP-1 Friendly)" is the clearest signal of where the line moved.
- Kind Patches is the most relevant comparable to FitPatches: same active (berberine), same format, and it scaled primarily via creators/TikTok Shop + bundles/subscription, not via a classic long advertorial.

### Gaps
- No public, verifiable teardown with copy and numbers was found for Lemme, Pendulum, Supergut, Akkermansia brands, Kreatures of Habit, Ritual, Hims/Hers or Ro weight-loss landing pages; searches for Kreatures of Habit and Sculptique advertorial breakdowns returned nothing specific.
- Arrae's listicle body and Pendulum's Meta LP could not be read (JS-rendered / not fetched); their headlines and offer blocks remain unverified.
- No brand-confirmed CVR/CTR numbers exist for any weight-loss advertorial in this set except network-level ClickBank stats for Mitolyn and course-seller claims for the Fixd double-advertorial.
- Java Burn, Lean Belly Juice, PatchAid, PatchMD, Lumi patches: not researched in depth due to tool budget; no teardown found in passing.

- Where numbers exist, the lift comes from (a) inserting a proof-heavy step before the offer and (b) raising AOV, not from a cheaper click.

### Gaps
- Exact CTA button copy and above-the-fold layouts are not public for most cases; only Kind Patches ("Add to Cart", "Subscribe & Save", "Find My Patch") was verified live.

## 3. Patch-specific: how weight-loss / berberine / "GLP-1" patches are presented, and what complaints say

### Takeaway
The market leader (Kind Patches) sells the patch on **convenience and consistency** ("peel, stick, go", "no pills, no powders", "zero strain on digestion", 8-hour wear, steady release vs pill "peaks & crashes") and has quietly retreated from explicit GLP-1/weight-loss claims; complaints cluster around "no results / how long until it works", skin irritation, adhesion, subscriptions and shipping — all objections that can be pre-empted in the advertorial.

### Cited Findings
How it is presented [VERIFIED-LIVE, Oct 2026 unless noted]:
- Lead copy: "Find it hard to maintain a wellness routine? Not any longer. Easy-to-apply Berberine Patches can be used daily – no pills, no complicated routines. Simply stick and go." — [Kind Patches product page](https://kindpatches.com/products/berberine-patches)
- Positioning as a helper, not a cure: "Minimal-effort patches to complement your weight loss journey… part of a healthy lifestyle… mindful eating, movement, hydration, and rest" — [Kind Patches product page](https://kindpatches.com/products/berberine-patches)
- Comparison table Pills vs Gummies vs Patches: "Peel & stick-done" vs "Swallow with water"; Digestive load "Zero strain on digestion" vs "Can upset stomach"; Bioavailability "High, due to direct absorption" vs "Often low due to gut breakdown"; Consistency "Smooth, steady release" vs "Peaks & Crashes"; "Travel-friendly, discreet, 8+ hour wear" — [Kind Patches product page](https://kindpatches.com/products/berberine-patches)
- Consistency/adherence argument: "Most people don't struggle with what to use. They struggle with sticking to it. Pills get skipped. Gummies get forgotten." — [Kind Patches: Science Behind Patches](https://kindpatches.com/pages/science-behind-patches)
- Ritual: clean dry skin → peel and stick → leave 8+ hours (or overnight) → remove gently; rotate placement; avoid lotions; don't stretch skin (prevents "tension blisters"); remove with water or oil — [Kind Patches: Science page](https://kindpatches.com/pages/science-behind-patches); [product page](https://kindpatches.com/products/berberine-patches)
- Trust devices: "Designed in Sweden", "100% Vegan and Cruelty-Free", "We don't outsource or white-label", ingredient-by-ingredient mg list, links to PubMed studies per ingredient, FAQ "Is there evidence…?" — [Kind Patches product page](https://kindpatches.com/products/berberine-patches); [Science page](https://kindpatches.com/pages/science-behind-patches)
- Benefit claims kept soft: "Wearable wellness patch / Steady support throughout the day / No side effects, discreet, simple"; "Good For: Weight management and daily wellness" — [Kind Patches product page](https://kindpatches.com/products/berberine-patches)

### Inferences
- Objections to pre-empt in a FitPatches advertorial (ranked by frequency in reviews): (1) "Does it actually work / how fast?" → set a realistic timeline and frame as support for appetite/cravings with habits; (2) "Will it irritate my skin?" → patch-test, rotation, removal-with-oil instructions, hypoallergenic adhesive if true; (3) "Does it stay on?" → show placement, showering, wear time; (4) "Is the dose enough through skin?" → expect this from educated readers given press coverage; avoid absorption/bioavailability superiority claims you cannot substantiate; (5) "Hidden subscription" → COD single payment is itself a trust advantage worth stating explicitly.
- Kind Patches' table claims ("High bioavailability, direct absorption", "Zero strain on digestion") are the kind of comparative claims that drew press fire; the safer, still-persuasive angle is the adherence argument ("pills get skipped").
- The "GLP-1" word is a proven attention magnet but the leader has demoted it to "GLP-1 Friendly"; in the EU this is additionally constrained by health-claims rules (covered by another researcher).

### Gaps
- No Reddit thread with substantive berberine-patch user experience surfaced in searches; Reddit sentiment remains unverified.
- No independent data on Kind Patches' Meta advertorials (if any) — its scale appears creator/TikTok-driven.
- PatchAid, PatchMD, Lumi and Mounjaro-branded patch sellers were not examined.

## 4. Patterns across winners: what recurs, and what differentiates top performers

### Takeaway
Nearly all winners combine a borrowed famous mechanism, a story/quiz that personalises the problem, a dense proof stack, and an offer engineered for AOV; top performers add a second pre-sell step (social-proof advertorial or quiz), scale creative through many testimonial/creator variants, and keep claims just inside what platforms tolerate.

### Cited Findings
Recurring elements (with examples):
- **Trending mechanism hook**: GLP-1 / "un-hunger hormone" (Lemme — [NLR](https://natlawreview.com/article/two-class-actions-target-supplement-promoted-increasing-glp-1)); "Ozempic-like results" (SkinnyFit — [FOTW](https://blog.funneloftheweek.com/p/8-figure-quiz-funnel-breakdown)); gut imbalance → weight gain (Bioma — [Biology Insights](https://biologyinsights.com/is-bioma-a-scam-the-truth-about-this-probiotic/)); lymph (Sculptique — [AdLibrarySpy](https://adlibraryspy.com/store/trysculptique.com))
- **Personalisation step before price**: SkinnyFit 12-question quiz; Noom 113 screens; Kind Patches "60 Second Quiz"; Bioma symptom quiz — [FOTW](https://blog.funneloftheweek.com/p/8-figure-quiz-funnel-breakdown); [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/web-to-app-onboarding-funnel); [Kind Patches](https://kindpatches.com)
- **Visual proof**: before/after (SkinnyFit 98 lbs; Keto Diet prelander; Harmonica) — [FOTW](https://blog.funneloftheweek.com/p/8-figure-quiz-funnel-breakdown); [LeadBit](https://blog.leadbit.com/successful-prelanding-for-nutra/); [CPA.RIP](https://cpa.rip/en/blogs/nutra-pre-landing-analysis/); "before/after pictures work extremely well" — [Traffic Cardinal](https://en.trafficcardinal.com/post/no-pain-no-gain-investigating-weight-loss-offers)
- **Authority**: doctor interview (Harmonica — [CPA.RIP](https://cpa.rip/en/blogs/nutra-pre-landing-analysis/)); HCP survey stats (Pendulum); "clinical trials across ingredients" (Arrae); PubMed links per ingredient (Kind Patches)
- **Social proof at scale**: review counts (Arrae 5,233; Kind 21k Trustpilot); "4 million customers"; dedicated social-proof advertorial (Fixd, ~80% CTR) — [Arrae](https://www.arrae.com/pages/mb-1-listicle); [Kind Patches](https://kindpatches.com); [FOTW](https://blog.funneloftheweek.com/p/2-advertorials-better-1)
- **AOV engineering**: $5 → $268 cart via 3 bumps + 5 upsells (SkinnyFit); Buy 2 Get 1 + bundles up to 50% off + subscribe-and-save (Kind); per-unit bundle pricing + continuity (Nooro); bottle bundles + one-click upsells (ClickBank) — sources above
- **Urgency**: limited-time/limited-supply (Harmonica, CPA.RIP); "discount valid 2 hours" (Traffic Cardinal); Nooro's personalised timeline dates instead of fake timers — [CPA.RIP](https://cpa.rip/en/blogs/nutra-pre-landing-analysis/); [Traffic Cardinal](https://en.trafficcardinal.com/post/no-pain-no-gain-investigating-weight-loss-offers); [FOTW AOV](https://blog.funneloftheweek.com/p/3-unconventional-ways-to-boost-your-aov)

Differentiators of top performers:
- **Extra pre-sell step** (second advertorial or quiz) — CPA −50% claimed in Fixd case — [FOTW](https://blog.funneloftheweek.com/p/2-advertorials-better-1)
- **Creative volume around one persona/testimonial** — Sculptique: 1,621 live Meta ads, ≥8 variants of one testimonial persona — [AdLibrarySpy](https://adlibraryspy.com/store/trysculptique.com); Lemme: 13,000+ creators, 53,000+ videos/quarter — [FemFounded](https://femfounded.org/case-studies/lemme/)
- **Context expansion**: weight-loss hook, but quiz/advertorial expands to skin/hair/energy benefits to raise perceived value — [FOTW](https://blog.funneloftheweek.com/p/8-figure-quiz-funnel-breakdown)
- **Localization (EU COD)**: native-language copy, locally relevant faces, quality certificates/eco labels valued by European audiences — [CPA.RIP](https://cpa.rip/en/blogs/nutra-pre-landing-analysis/); [LeadBit](https://blog.leadbit.com/successful-prelanding-for-nutra/)

Risk pattern (losers / casualties):
- Claim aggressiveness has consequences: Lemme class actions — [Truth in Advertising](https://truthinadvertising.org/class-action/lemme-glp-1-daily/); Kind Patches forced rename and negative press — [TODAY.com](https://www.today.com/health/diet-fitness/glp-1-patches-rcna232201); FDA reportedly issued 80+ warning letters to telehealth companies over compounded-GLP-1 marketing claims in 2025–2026 [from a search summary; primary FDA list not fetched — verify before quoting] — [Kelley Drye Ad Law Access](https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/ad-law-access/taking-a-shot-at-glp-1-weight-loss)

### Inferences
- For a COD berberine patch in Bulgaria, the transferable "winner" stack is: (1) GLP-1-adjacent but compliant mechanism framing (appetite/cravings/blood-sugar balance), (2) a story or doctor-led advertorial with a short self-check/quiz element, (3) a separate proof block or second page of local reviews/before-afters, (4) bundle ladder (1/2+1/3+2 type) shown per-unit, (5) COD as risk reversal.
- Testimonial persona multiplication (Sculptique) and creator volume (Lemme/Kind) suggest creative volume matters more than any single advertorial's polish.

### Gaps
- No controlled A/B data comparing listicle vs story vs doctor formats for weight loss specifically was found.

## 5. European nutra CPA network "prelanding + landing" examples (COD weight loss, Eastern Europe)

### Takeaway
EU COD weight-loss funnels run by Dr.Cash/LeadBit/Everad affiliates are built on network-supplied prelanders (news/story/doctor interview with before-afters and comment-style reviews) feeding a landing page with a short name+phone form; public cases report 67–161% ROI on Facebook with ~50% approval rates, but they are mostly 2019–2020 and the sector has a documented history of fake doctors/celebrities, which is both a compliance and a trust risk.

### Cited Findings
- **Ideal Slim (Italy, Dr.Cash, Facebook)**: Apr 1–17, 2020; spend $9,400 → revenue $20,700 → profit $11,300 (ROI 120%); broadest targeting (women 30+); used the network's pre-landing (ID 11351), improved before launch; follow-up with Weight Manager in Poland [CLAIMED by affiliate; 2020] — [AffiliateFix Dr.Cash thread (search summary; page returned 403)](https://www.affiliatefix.com/threads/dr-cash-health-beauty-affiliate-network-nutra-exclusive-offers-tips-tricks-and-cases.156354/page-2)
- **KETO Garcinia (Romania, Facebook, prelanding + landing)**: 9 days; spend $1,328 → earnings $3,473 (ROI ~161%); lead cost rose from $0.70 to $9 [CLAIMED] — [Traffic Cardinal](https://en.trafficcardinal.com/post/no-pain-no-gain-investigating-weight-loss-offers)
- **Dietonus (Spain)**: 13 days; $1,797 → $4,579 (ROI ~155%); CPC ~$0.10; CTR 4–5%; lead cost $1.40 [CLAIMED] — [Traffic Cardinal](https://en.trafficcardinal.com/post/no-pain-no-gain-investigating-weight-loss-offers)
- **Multi-offer slimming (CIS/Europe)**: 20 days; $120,000 → $200,000 (ROI ~67%); CPM ~$1 [CLAIMED] — [Traffic Cardinal](https://en.trafficcardinal.com/post/no-pain-no-gain-investigating-weight-loss-offers)
- **Offers available for Bulgaria** (illustrative of what BG shoppers see): Reduslim (BG, IT, ES, DE, AT, GR, CY, HU…), 7 Slim Active (RO, SK, BG, HR, PL, …; payout $20–31) — [Traffic Cardinal](https://en.trafficcardinal.com/post/no-pain-no-gain-investigating-weight-loss-offers); Everad lists slimming offers for Bulgaria, Romania, Poland, etc., COD with phone confirmation — [Incogniton on Everad](https://incogniton.com/blog/getting-into-everad-everything-you-need-to-know/); [Affbank on Everad](https://affbank.com/blog/how-to-work-with-nutra-vertical-on-everad)
- **Keto Diet CZ (LeadBit)**: 52% approval rate; three-block prelander — (1) hook headline with before/after ("Catherine Diggie Achieved Incredible Results in 24 Weeks"), (2) desire-building with pain-point photos (struggling to fasten clothes) contrasted with fitted-clothes results, (3) customer reviews; European audiences respond to quality certificates and eco labels [CLAIMED by network] — [LeadBit blog](https://blog.leadbit.com/successful-prelanding-for-nutra/)
- **Harmonica (Turkey, COD) doctor-interview prelander** block order: menu/language → doctor interview with specialist quote → product benefits → testimonials + before/after → limited-time/supply offer → CTA. Strengths: localisation, problem-prevalence statistics, objection handling via Q&A. Weaknesses flagged: no study citations, interview lacks a conclusion leading to reviews, inconsistent photos, sweepstakes format instead of direct order form, faces not matching local demographics — [CPA.RIP analysis](https://cpa.rip/en/blogs/nutra-pre-landing-analysis/)
- **Standard landing blocks** for these offers: attention headline, composition, results list, bright order button or callback, testimonials, time limit ("discount valid 2 hours"), company data; prelander in "article form" telling the product story and handling objections; angles: celebrity stories, doctor/nutritionist, "conspiracy" (what the industry hides), seasonal ("lose weight by summer") — [Traffic Cardinal](https://en.trafficcardinal.com/post/no-pain-no-gain-investigating-weight-loss-offers)
- **COD benchmark (non-weight-loss but same funnel)**: Erogan in IT/RO/CZ via Facebook (Aug–Nov 2019) — spend $19,600 → revenue $49,046 (ROI 150%), 55% approval across three countries, network prelanders "processed a bit" — [AffiliateValley](https://affiliatevalley.com/case-studies/affiliate-marketing-case-study-how-to-earn-almost-30k-on-facebook-ads/)
- **Cautionary investigation (Everad)**: Romanian outlet Recorder (verified by AIN.UA, Mar 2021) documented fake landing pages mimicking health-ministry sites, stolen identities of doctors and celebrities (e.g., Vladimir Posner shown as "Dr. Ionescu Octavian"), stock-photo testimonials, call-centre staff urging elderly people to drop prescribed treatment; Everad revenue grew from $208k (2017) to $16.5M (2019); LeadBit and MGID cited as distribution partners — [AIN.UA](https://en.ain.ua/2021/03/17/everad-heads-massive-scam/)

### Inferences
- The EU COD prelander template (story or doctor interview → before/after → comment-style reviews → limited-time 50% discount → name+phone form) is the "native language" of Bulgarian weight-loss shoppers, so FitPatches competes visually with these pages; differentiation should come from verifiable elements (real Bulgarian customers, real pharmacist/doctor with consent, real address/company data) rather than copying fabricated authority.
- Approval rate (~50–55% in network cases) is the hidden COD metric: prelander honesty (price, what arrives, no "miracle") likely improves approval/redemption, though no source measured this directly.
- Lead cost inflation in the Keto Garcinia case ($0.70 → $9 in 9 days) shows how fast a single prelander/creative burns out on Facebook in small EU geos.

### Gaps
- No 2022–2026 COD weight-loss case with spend/revenue numbers was found; all numeric EU cases located are 2019–2020.
- No Bulgaria-specific prelander teardown was found (other researchers cover the Bulgarian market).
- EverAd, KMA.biz, M1-shop, Terraleads and AdCombo blogs were not reached in this pass; spy-tool (AdHeart, SpyOver, Anstrex) screenshots of current BG slimming prelanders were not accessible.
