# Bulgarian (and CEE) Market Specifics for Weight-Loss Advertorials — FitPatches (berberine patches, BG, women 35–60, Meta → advertorial → COD offer page)

Research date: 3 Oct 2026. Data years are flagged inline. "Inference" = my reasoning, not a sourced fact.

## Q1. Bulgarian online consumer behavior: COD vs card, couriers/lockers, COD refusal rates, mobile, social media use by women 35–60

### Takeaway
Cash on delivery is still the single biggest payment method in Bulgarian e-commerce (~48% of payments in 2025, down from 50.3% in 2024), and 92.9% of BG stores offer it. Bulgarians mostly collect parcels from a courier office or locker rather than at home (70.1%). The audience is mobile-first (~67% of web traffic). Facebook reaches ~1.2M women aged 35–64. The main financial risk in a COD nutra funnel is the unclaimed or refused parcel, so the page has to sell a clear, final price before the click and set expectations about the confirmation call and the courier.

### Cited Findings
**Payments**
- BEA (Bulgarian E-commerce Association) annual survey, 2025 edition based on 4.95M shipments from members: COD share keeps falling and "reaches about 48% of all payments" in 2025. Card payment share is rising — [Bloomberg TV Bulgaria, 16 Dec 2025](https://www.bloombergtv.bg/a/17-v-razvitie/153567-pazarat-na-elektronna-targoviya-u-nas-e-sred-nay-barzo-rastyashtite-v-iztochna-evropa); same text in [BIA](https://www.bia-bg.com/news/view/35146/)
- BEA 2024 ("Паспорт на е-търговията", 8th edition): COD 50.30%, credit/debit cards 38.20%, bank and mobile payments 7.29%, BNPL 4.21% — [BIA: "Идва ли краят на наложения платеж?"](https://www.bia-bg.com/news/view/35112/)
- BEA chair Zhanet Naydenova (Dec 2025) called the viral claim that COD is ending "misleading". She also said merchants would welcome all-digital payments because COD creates "a huge problem: unclaimed parcels" (невзетите пратки). At the current rate of decline, COD will take "decades" to disappear — [BNR Horizont fact-check, 9 Dec 2025](https://bnrnews.bg/horizont/post/397516/zadava-li-se-krayat-na-nalozheniya-platezh)
- In the same piece, the consumer platform "Ние, потребителите" describes COD as a consumer guarantee against the "many fake sites", meaning you pay only when you have the goods — [BNR, 9 Dec 2025](https://bnrnews.bg/horizont/post/397516/zadava-li-se-krayat-na-nalozheniya-platezh)
- 92.9% of Bulgarian online stores offer COD. 77%+ accept cards, 58%+ bank transfer, 26% BNPL and 18.1% digital wallets. Source: Balkan Ecommerce Summit, "State of e-commerce in the Balkans 2025" (BG, RO, GR, HR, HU) — [DIGIPAY, 26 Feb 2026](https://digipay.bg/bg/blog/e-commerce-92-9-of-online-stores-in-bulgaria-offer-cash-on-delivery)
- **Conflicting figure:** BOX NOW's press release on the КРС (Communications Regulation Commission) 2025 annual report says "fewer than one in three consumers choose COD". It adds that card payments are growing, "including on COD purchases", i.e. paying the courier or locker by card — [Business Новините/bTV, 24 Aug 2026](https://businessnovinite.bg/bg-biznes/vsjaka-chetvarta-pratka-ot-pokupki-onlajn-se-dostavja-do-avtomat-u-nas.html). BEA's 48% counts merchants' shipments, while the КРС/BOX NOW figure appears to be consumer-level. Treat ~1/3–1/2 as the plausible range. Other sources put COD at "60–80% of e-commerce orders" in BG and the Balkans, with no methodology given — [XLOG.BG 2026 guide](https://xlog.bg/nalozhen-platezh-za-onlayn-magazini/). That is likely overstated for general e-commerce but plausible for impulse nutra.
- 2025 market: online sales ~€2.69B (+15% y/y). 55% of Bulgarians aged 16–74 shop online, and 95.5% of them buy from Bulgarian stores. Online is ~8% of retail. BEA survey, Aug–Oct 2025 — [BTA, 10 Dec 2025](https://www.bta.bg/bg/news/economy/1025127-onlayn-prodazhbite-na-produkti-i-uslugi-v-stranata-rastat-po-prognozni-danni-s-1)

**Couriers, offices, lockers**
- Econt, Speedy and DHL Express together hold 79.7% of the courier market. Econt is #1 and Speedy #2. The courier market was ~1B BGN in 2024. Source: КРС 2024 data — [24plovdiv, 30 Jun 2025](https://www.24plovdiv.bg/novini/article/20815802)
- КРС annual report 2025: 83% of consumers use courier services, and 70.1% prefer delivery to a courier office or locker rather than to their address. About 1 in 4 e-commerce parcels now goes to a locker. Lockers totalled 3,725 at end-2025 (+18% y/y), and BOX NOW had 795 — [Business Новините, 24 Aug 2026](https://businessnovinite.bg/bg-biznes/vsjaka-chetvarta-pratka-ot-pokupki-onlajn-se-dostavja-do-avtomat-u-nas.html)
- Network sizes (July 2026): Econt has 550+ offices but only 38 lockers. Speedy has 1,200+ points including 800+ lockers. Sameday easybox had 950+ lockers (Nov 2024). BOX NOW has ~800 and plans 1,200 by end-2026. Locker deliveries are estimated at 25–30% of deliveries — [MobileBulgaria, 7 Jul 2026](https://mobilebulgaria.com/news/avtomatite-prenarezhdat-kurierskiya-pazar-a-ekont-ostava-nazad)
- COD fees for merchants: about 0.80–2 BGN fixed plus 1–2% of value. Econt is ≈1.5–1.8% incl. VAT and Speedy ≈1.5–2% — [XLOG.BG, 2026](https://xlog.bg/nalozhen-platezh-za-onlayn-magazini/)
- Econt's "Преглед и тест" (inspect and test) service lets the recipient inspect or try the goods and hand the parcel straight back. The merchant decides who pays outbound and return shipping if it is refused — [Econt developer docs: Преглед и тест](https://www.econt.com/developers/26-pregled-i-test.html) (from search summary; page not opened)

**Refusals and returns**
- BEA: 7.1% of all online orders were returned under the 14-day withdrawal right in 2024 (7.99% in 2023). Clothing, shoes and accessories had the highest rate at 15% — [BIA](https://www.bia-bg.com/news/view/35112/). Note: another summary quotes 32.9% for clothing ([search summary of BIA/BNR results](https://www.bia-bg.com/news/view/35112/)), so treat the category number as uncertain. These figures are *returns*, not COD *refusals at the door*.
- Benchmarks for refusals by category (no methodology stated): cosmetics 5–10%, home 8–15%, sport 10–18%, fashion 15–25%. Lockers are said to have lower refusal rates because of the longer pickup window — [XLOG.BG, 2026](https://xlog.bg/nalozhen-platezh-za-onlayn-magazini/)
- Monthly refusals above ~5% typically wipe out the profit from paid ads, because courier, packaging and labour are already spent. Main causes: no confirmation, inaccurate data, slow delivery, confusion about amounts and fees, impulse purchases. Recommended fixes:
  - SMS confirmation link before dispatch
  - Phone validation by SMS code
  - Office or courier pick-list (instead of free-text address)
  - Up to 4 SMS per order (confirmation → pre-dispatch → delivery-day window → failed attempt)
  - For high-risk orders (new customer, >200 BGN, address with past refusals): a 10 BGN card prepayment, or office-only delivery
  - Target: −20% refusals within 30 days
  
  — [Bezraboten.net, 13 Nov 2025](https://bezraboten.net/onlayn-magazini-kak-da-namalite-otkazani-nalozheni-plashtaniya/)

**Mobile and social**
- Web traffic share, Sept 2026: mobile 66.88%, desktop 32.28%, tablet 0.85% — [StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/platform-market-share/desktop-mobile-tablet/bulgaria)
- DataReportal Digital 2026 Bulgaria (data Oct 2025, published 8 Nov 2025):
  - Population 6.70M, median age 44.8; internet users 5.84M (87.1%)
  - YouTube 4.50M; Facebook ad reach 3.60M (53.7% of pop., −2.7% y/y, 51.4% female); Messenger 2.80M
  - TikTok 18+ 2.63M (+21.4% y/y, **61.4% female**); Instagram 1.95M (53% female)
  
  — [DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-bulgaria)
- NapoleonCat, Sept 2026: 4,460,700 Facebook users in BG (67.1% of pop.), 51.9% women. Age split by total share: 18–24 11.3%, 25–34 21.8%, 35–44 20.3%, 45–54 18.9%, 55–64 14.3%, 65+ 13.4%. The page's women/men columns are offset by one row; re-aligned so that each pair sums to the row total, women are 35–44 ≈ 9.9%, 45–54 ≈ 9.6%, 55–64 ≈ 7.8% and 65+ ≈ 8.3% of users. That gives ≈1.2M women aged 35–64 on Facebook. The page text also says women's biggest lead is in 65+ (by 229,100). Monthly totals swing between 4.32M and 4.73M, so these are ad-audience estimates — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/stats/facebook-users-in-bulgaria/)
- NapoleonCat, Sept 2026: 2,115,400 Instagram users (31.8%), 53.7% women. Women's largest lead is in the 45–54 band (+143,400). The per-age gender numbers on that page did not parse reliably — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/stats/instagram-users-in-bulgaria/)

### Inferences
- For women 35–60, Facebook (feed and Reels) is the core channel and Instagram is secondary. TikTok is growing fast and skews female, so it is worth testing for the 35–45 end of the audience.
- The offer page should present COD as the default and add an optional card payment ("плащане с карта при получаване" or online). Don't push prepayment on cold traffic: COD is how this audience reduces risk, especially after the scam stories (Q3).
- The order form should default to **office or locker delivery**: a pick-list of Econt offices, Speedy offices or lockers, and BOX NOW lockers. Lockers give a longer pickup window and fewer awkward doorstep refusals. Econt barely has lockers, so for locker pickup offer Speedy or BOX NOW.
- Within the first hour, send an SMS (or Viber) with the order number, the exact EUR total including delivery, the courier and the expected date. A short confirmation call ("потвърждение на поръчката") is still standard in BG nutra. The page should warn: "Ще Ви се обадим за потвърждение от номер +359…".
- An "Преглед" (inspect before paying) option is cheap insurance for a patch product. For a consumable, "тест" makes less sense; "преглед на опаковката" is enough.

### Gaps
- No public, credible figure for **COD refusal or buyout rates specifically for nutra/supplements in Bulgaria**. Courier companies don't publish refusal rates by category.
- I did not find Econt's or Speedy's own public reports with COD shares.
- Eurostat's 2025 ICT-usage figure for internet purchases by women 35–64 in BG was not retrieved.

## Q2. Health attitudes: doctors vs system, folk remedies and supplements, obesity and diabetes prevalence, GLP-1 access and prices, berberine awareness and regulation

### Takeaway
Bulgarians like their own GP but distrust the system and pay for medicine out of pocket at the highest rate in the EU. Self-medication is normal: about 40% favour folk medicine, and 35–38% have bought health products because of ads. Self-reported adult obesity is low (12%), but inactivity is the worst in the EU and diabetes affects ~770k people (half undiagnosed). This makes "blood sugar / insulin resistance / sugar cravings" a stronger hook than BMI. GLP-1 drugs cost €200–475 a month out of pocket for weight loss and are reimbursed only for type 2 diabetes, which leaves room for a "natural, affordable alternative" frame. The catch: EFSA said in 2026 that it cannot establish a safe intake level for berberine.

### Cited Findings
**Health system and trust**
- Out-of-pocket (OOP) payments were 36.0% of all health spending in 2023, the highest in the EU and more than double the EU average of 16%. Medicines make up over two-thirds of OOP spending. Retail pharmaceuticals were 31% of health spending, the highest in the EU (EU average 13%). Public insurance covered only ~23% of retail drug spending, the lowest in the EU. In 2018, 19% of households faced catastrophic health spending, about 3× the EU average — [OECD/EU State of Health, Country Health Profile 2025: Bulgaria (Dec 2025)](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/12/country-health-profile-2025-country-notes_7e72146d/bulgaria_242bb908/cd5706eb-en.pdf)
- The same report finds 36% of the population with poor or unsatisfactory health literacy, plus "low institutional trust" and misinformation, especially since COVID — [OECD/EU 2025](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/12/country-health-profile-2025-country-notes_7e72146d/bulgaria_242bb908/cd5706eb-en.pdf)
- Trend survey (2017, n=1,004): 40% prefer to treat themselves with folk medicine (народна медицина), ~50% among people over 60 and 25% among the youngest. 96% know their GP and 90% are satisfied with them. 75% see a doctor only when in pain, and 30% prefer alternative medicine — [Manager.bg](https://manager.bg/obshchestvo/40-ot-blgarite-razchitat-na-narodnata-medicina) *(2017 data)*
- Trend survey for the "Здраве без риск" campaign, backed by the Ministry of Health, the Bulgarian Drug Agency (ИАЛ) and the Bulgarian Medical Association (БЛС); n=1,000, face-to-face, published 3 Sep 2026:
  - 38% have used a medicine, supplement or other product advertised as good for health
  - 35% bought one under the influence of ads, internet posts or recommendations
  - 16% never consult a doctor or pharmacist first (~800k people)
  - 40% used products recommended by beauticians, influencers or trainers
  - ~40% don't check the composition
  - **Women are more susceptible to health-product advertising than men**
  
  — [BTA](https://www.bta.bg/bg/news/bulgaria/1197265-dva-miliona-balgari-sa-izpolzvali-produkt-reklamiran-kato-polezen-za-zdraveto-)
- 39% rarely or never consult a doctor before taking an advertised medicine or supplement — [Светът на здравето](https://svetatnazdraveto.bg/novini/39-ot-balgarite-kupuvat-dobavki-i-lekarstva-bez-konsultacija-s-lekar.html)
- A CPA-industry GEO review (Russian) says paid and insurance-based medicine and poor access to some services push Bulgarians toward self-treatment with supplements and OTC products. Main nutra pains: diabetes, joints, hypertension, vision, prostate and potency, with weight and beauty growing. It also says Russian is understood by people over 55 — [GdeTraffic, 21 Feb 2025](https://gdetraffic.com/Analitika/Bolgariya_obzor_GEO_Kak_profitno_lit_na_Bolgariyu)

**Weight and metabolic prevalence**
- Adult obesity was 12% in 2022 (self-reported, EU-SILC), the 3rd lowest in the EU. It is 14% among people without upper secondary education and 8% among graduates. Only 11% of adults are physically active ≥3 times a week, the lowest in the EU. Only 34% eat fruit daily and 43% vegetables daily. 24% of 15-year-olds are overweight or obese (2022) — [OECD/EU 2025](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/12/country-health-profile-2025-country-notes_7e72146d/bulgaria_242bb908/cd5706eb-en.pdf)
- EU context for 2025: obesity 16.3% (men 17.4%, women 15.3%) and pre-obesity 35.4%. Malta is highest at 26.6%; Bulgaria is not named in the release — [Eurostat, 4 Sep 2026](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20260904-1)
- Diabetes (Bulgarian Society of Endocrinology press conference, 22 Oct 2024):
  - ~770,000 people with type 2 diabetes in 2024, up from 487,000 in 2006; **~50% undiagnosed**
  - Every third person over 60 has diabetes
  - 235,000+ people with prediabetes (2021 IDF figure), 80% of them unaware
  
  — [Bgonair](https://www.bgonair.bg/a/2-bulgaria/361684-polovinata-ot-horata-s-diabet-tip-2-ne-podozirat-za-sastoyanieto-si). Other sources give 482k (2024) — [Utro Ruse](https://utroruse.com/article/1159338/). The numbers conflict depending on whether they come from registry or screening.

**GLP-1 access and prices**
- НЗОК (the National Health Insurance Fund) covers Ozempic only for non-insulin-dependent (type 2) diabetes, not obesity — [ForLife, 6 Mar 2026](https://www.forlife.bg/nyama-da-ima-inzhekczii-za-otslabvane-za-vsichki). Off-label use for weight loss is paid entirely by the patient — [Diabetico](https://diabetico.bg/cena-na-ozempik-v-bulgaria/) (undated)
- Ozempic shortage, May 2023: a 3-month delivery delay caused by Novo Nordisk production problems. Shortages hit periodically, especially small towns. ~50 medicines were under export bans — [Сега, 19 May 2023](https://www.segabg.com/category-zdrave/ozempik-shte-lipsva-tri-meseca-bulgarskite-apteki) *(2023)*. A later search snippet says semaglutide was on export-restriction lists "over the past year" — [SafeNews](https://safenews.bg/darzhavata-zaklyuchi-lekarstvata-palna-blokada-na-iznosa-do-2026-g-ozempik-pod-klyuch/) (page could not be opened; unverified).
- Mounjaro (tirzepatide) pharmacy prices in 2026: ~€204.56 for 2.5 mg, €273.47 for 5 mg and €475.07 for 15 mg (4-dose pen, i.e. 1 month). Prescription only — [MoiteApteki / Framar / pharmacy search results](https://moiteapteki.bg/lekarstvo/mounjaro/)
- Wegovy: ~€160 (0.25–1 mg), ~€200 (1.7 mg), ~€225 (2.4 mg) per pen. Ozempic 1 mg pen ~89.78 BGN (≈€45.90) — [Diabetico (undated)](https://diabetico.bg/cena-na-ozempik-v-bulgaria/)

**Berberine: market and regulatory status**
- Berberine is a mainstream pharmacy SKU in BG. Framar lists Efime (60 caps €18.45), Nixen (500 mg × 90, €23.95), Haya Labs (€18.41), Dr. Green "Берберин Слим" (€24.54) and Thorne (€35.65), among others — [Framar search results](https://apteka.framar.bg/30127439/%D0%B1%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B1%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BD-%D0%BA%D0%B0%D0%BF%D1%81%D1%83%D0%BB%D0%B8-500-%D0%BC%D0%B3-90-%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%BA%D1%81%D0%B5%D0%BD). Revita.bg carries 25 berberine products at ~€19–€94, all capsules, positioned on "blood sugar, cholesterol, metabolic balance, weight management" — [Revita.bg](https://revita.bg/by-category/berberin)
- **EFSA:** in January 2026 the NDA Panel endorsed a draft opinion (Art. 8, Reg. 1925/2006) that could not establish a safe intake level for berberine-containing preparations. It cited signals of genotoxicity, rodent carcinogenicity, liver injury, hypoglycaemia, hypotension and drug interactions. If berberine is later listed in Annex III Part A, it would be prohibited in foods and supplements — [NutraIngredients, 10 Mar 2026](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2026/03/10/no-safe-intake-level-for-berberine-efsa-opens-consultation/); [Freyr](https://www.freyrsolutions.com/consumer-industry-news/efsa-opens-consultation-on-berberine-safety-opinion-draft-finds-safe-intake-could-not-be-established)
- The consultation deadline was given as 4 May 2026 by NutraIngredients and 10 July 2026 by another summary (possibly extended). As of 31 Jul 2026 the regulatory status was still being decided, with talk of "tighter restrictions, or even a ban" — [NutraIngredients, 31 Jul 2026](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2026/07/31/safety-in-question-what-is-the-future-of-berberine/). The status was still unresolved in coverage dated 2 Oct 2026 — [NutraIngredients, 2 Oct 2026](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2026/10/02/botanical-safety-is-article-8-fit-for-purpose/)

### Inferences
- **Angle fit:** Bulgarian women 35–60 are under-diagnosed for blood-sugar problems, inactive, and used to self-treating with herbs and supplements. "Insulin resistance / sugar cravings / belly fat after 40 / menopause" angles fit better than "obesity". The "natural Ozempic alternative" frame works because GLP-1s cost ~€200+ a month and aren't reimbursed for weight loss. Avoid saying the product *equals* Ozempic. Frame it as "растителна съставка, изследвана за подкрепа на нормалните нива на кръвната захар".
- **Authority:** trust sits with the personal GP and pharmacist, not "the system" or the state. A real, named Bulgarian pharmacist or nutritionist who consented on camera ("магистър-фармацевт") is a strong signal. The "doctor the system doesn't want you to know about" trope is now the scam signature (Q3).
- **Risk:** the EFSA opinion is a reputational and regulatory exposure. Copy should avoid "100% безопасно, без странични ефекти". It should include short contraindications (pregnancy, people on diabetes medication, hypoglycaemia risk) and "консултирайте се с лекар, ако приемате лекарства". This makes the page look like a legitimate brand, unlike the scam pages, which never carry contraindications.

### Gaps
- No measured (rather than self-reported) obesity data for BG women 35–60 from WHO or НЦОЗА (the National Centre of Public Health and Analyses) was retrieved. EU-SILC self-reports probably understate prevalence.
- No Google Trends or survey data on Bulgarian public **awareness of berberine** was found. The proxy is that it is widely stocked in pharmacies.
- The current НЗОК protocol text for GLP-1s and any 2026 shortage notices from ИАЛ were not retrieved.
- Whether transdermal patches count as a food supplement, cosmetic or medical device in BG (БАБХ, the Bulgarian Food Safety Agency, vs ИАЛ) was not researched. This matters for which claims rules apply.

## Q3. Trust signals: what works for Bulgarians vs what is now associated with scams

### Takeaway
Bulgaria has just lived through a widely covered scam bust. In May 2026, a €240–250M network was taken down that used AI deepfakes of well-known doctors and TV journalists, fake news pages and call centres to sell supplements. Since 2019 it had reached millions of customers across BG/RO/MD/PL/HU. Fake TV/news-site prelanders, celebrity or famous-doctor endorsements, "miracle" claims, empty-profile comments and foreign phone numbers are now the scam signature named by КЗП (the Consumer Protection Commission), bTV, BNT and fact-checkers. What still builds trust is anything *verifiable and local*: a Bulgarian company with an ЕИК, a +359 phone, real addresses, a named and consenting specialist, real customer UGC, COD with inspection, and EUR prices.

### Cited Findings
**The 2026 bust**
- On 18 May 2026, Bulgarian services (Sofia Regional Prosecutor's Office, the cybercrime directorate ГДБОП, the Interior Ministry) and partners took down an international network selling fake supplements:
  - Revenue €240–250M; active since ~2019; 300+ product types; 10 production sites in BG
  - Sold through **call centres**, using **AI-generated videos with the faces of well-known doctors and journalists**
  - Targeted elderly people with hypertension and cardiovascular problems; products presented as medicines
  - Arrests in BG (4 foreign nationals), Romania (6) and Poland (1)
  
  — [Dnes.bg](https://www.dnes.bg/a/6-krimi/721821-prestapnata-shema-za-hranitelni-dobavki-e-izpolzvala-ai-i-falshivi-reklami); [Dnevnik](https://www.dnevnik.bg/bulgaria/2026/05/18/4915565_bulgarskite_slujbi_razbiha_mejdunarodna_mreja_za/); [BNT](https://bnt.bg/news/akciya-na-balgarskite-sluzhbi-razbiha-mezhdunarodna-mreja-za-falshivi-hranitelni-dobavki-vladimir-dimitrov-i-prof-ivo-petrov-v415480-348994news.html?page=71); [Focus](https://www.focus-news.net/novini/Bylgaria/Falshivi-reklami-na-lekarstva-Nad-300-vida-nelegalni-hranitelni-dobavki-zalivat-pazara-ustanoveni-sa-proizvodstveni-pomeshteniya-u-nas-2967647)
- The investigation began after a **former health minister** found his name and photo in misleading ads. Other well-known doctors joined. Ads claimed products "clean blood vessels" and cure serious diseases — [Dnes.bg](https://www.dnes.bg/a/6-krimi/721821-prestapnata-shema-za-hranitelni-dobavki-e-izpolzvala-ai-i-falshivi-reklami); [search summary of multiple outlets](https://eurocom.bg/2026/05/18/prokuraturata-i-mvr-razbiha-mrezha-za-izmami-s-dobavki-za-240-mln-evro)

**Names and formats abused**
- Cardiologist **Prof. Ivo Petrov** appeared in an AI video with "В фокус" presenter **Laura Krumova**, promoting "his" diabetes product supposedly created at "Vienna University". He has reported such fakes repeatedly — [Zdrave.net](https://www.zdrave.net/%D0%9D%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D0%BD%D0%B8/%D0%9F%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%B4%D0%BD%D0%B0-%D1%84%D0%B0%D0%BB%D1%88%D0%B8%D0%B2%D0%B0-%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%BA%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%BC%D0%B0--%D0%9F%D1%80%D0%BE%D1%84.-%D0%9F%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2-%D1%81%D1%8A%D1%81--%D1%81%D0%B2%D0%BE%D0%B9-%D0%BF%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B4%D1%83%D0%BA%D1%82--%D0%B7%D0%B0-%D0%BB%D0%B5%D1%87%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B5-%D0%BD%D0%B0-%D0%B4%D0%B8%D0%B0%D0%B1%D0%B5%D1%82/n32581); [Blitz](https://blitz.bg/obshtestvo/vnimanie-razprostranyavat-falshiva-reklama-na-lekarstvo-ot-imeto-na-izvesten-profesor_news907293.html)
- Other abused faces reported by bTV and others:

This predates the 2026 bust and its media coverage.

**Signals that still work (legitimate)**
- COD itself is seen as protection against fake sites — [BNR, Dec 2025](https://bnrnews.bg/horizont/post/397516/zadava-li-se-krayat-na-nalozheniya-platezh)
- Legitimate BG patch and supplement sellers show delivery via Econt/Speedy within 48h, COD, a named holistic centre with specialists, ingredient lists, a "results may vary" disclaimer and links to their own social accounts, in formal "Вие" — [Vita Vivere Slim Style patches](https://vitavivere.com/slim-style)

### Inferences
**Now hurts trust (avoid):**
- Pages styled as bTV, Nova, 24 Часа, Blitz or Dnevnik news; TV logos; fake domains
- Any real celebrity, TV presenter or "famous professor"; AI-voiced talking heads of "doctors"
- "Invented in Vienna/Germany"; "cures diabetes"; "clean vessels"
- Fake comment threads with stock faces; fake stock counters ("остават 23 пакета"); "само днес" with today's date auto-inserted
- Foreign or hidden phone numbers; BGN-only prices after Aug 2026 (see Q5)

A "neutral editorial" advertorial (first-person story or "health column") is still fine, as long as it is clearly branded or labelled "Реклама / Платена публикация" and doesn't impersonate a media outlet. Meta also blocks impersonation.

**Still builds trust (use):**
- A Bulgarian legal entity in the footer: name, **ЕИК**, address, and a **+359** landline or mobile that is answered in Bulgarian
- Courier logos (Econt/Speedy/BOX NOW) and "Плащане при доставка, с право на преглед"
- A named, real Bulgarian pharmacist or nutritionist who consents to appear (photo plus role, ideally a short selfie video), plus a 14-day withdrawal statement
- Customer UGC from real buyers: first name plus initial, age and Bulgarian city (Пловдив, Варна, Бургас, Русе, Стара Загора); unpolished phone photos and short videos
- Contraindications, an ingredient panel, and a link to the brand's real FB/IG pages with comment history
- Optionally, "Как да разпознаете фалшивите реклами" turned into a differentiator: "Ние никога не използваме лица на известни личности. Всички отзиви са от реални клиенти." This turns the scam backlash into a positioning advantage.

Testimonials will be judged harder than before. Too-perfect stock photos and identical review texts are exactly the pattern Bulgarians have been told to look for.

- No source confirmed the specific Kamen Donev or Nikolay Banev fake-endorsement cases mentioned in the brief. The search returned other names (Ivo Petrov, Laura Krumova, Gabrovski, Viktoria Petrova, Kunchev, Nenkov, Tencheva).
- No quantitative study (e.g. A/B tests) of which trust elements lift conversion in BG was found. Recommendations here are inferred.
- I did not research Meta's 2026 enforcement against health deepfakes in BG (ad rejections or account bans).

## Q4. Language and tone: "Вие" vs "ти", humour, length, typical phrasing

### Takeaway
For health offers aimed at women 35–60, formal "Вие" (capitalised when addressing the reader directly) is the safe default. Legitimate BG patch sellers and even CPA pages use it. A first-person testimonial narrator can speak informally. The audience reads long stories if they are personal and plain, but health literacy is low, so short paragraphs and plain words matter.

### Cited Findings
- Bulgarian copywriting debate: "на Вие" is the safe form, and a good copywriter can weave in colloquial phrases. Women increasingly expect respect as independent buyers, which rules out addressing unknown women with "ти". "Ти" closes distance and feels like a one-to-one conversation — [LinkedIn, Martin Tarpev: "По-учтива или по-friendly форма в копирайтинга"](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/%D0%BF%D0%BE-%D1%83%D1%87%D1%82%D0%B8%D0%B2%D0%B0-%D0%B8%D0%BB%D0%B8-%D0%BF%D0%BE-friendly-%D1%84%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%BC%D0%B0-%D0%B2-%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BF%D0%B8%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B9%D1%82%D0%B8%D0%BD%D0%B3%D0%B0-martin-tarpev)
- Observed usage: the legitimate patch page (Vita Vivere Slim Style) uses formal "Вие" throughout — [Vita Vivere](https://vitavivere.com/slim-style). A CPA offer page (Easyloss BG) also uses formal "Вие", with "Изчакайте обаждането от оператора" — [Easyloss BG](https://easyloss.shop/bulgaria/)
- 36% of Bulgarians have poor or unsatisfactory health literacy, highest among older and lower-SES groups — [OECD/EU 2025](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/12/country-health-profile-2025-country-notes_7e72146d/bulgaria_242bb908/cd5706eb-en.pdf)
- Russian is understood by the 55+ cohort; Bulgarian is the primary language — [GdeTraffic, 2025](https://gdetraffic.com/Analitika/Bolgariya_obzor_GEO_Kak_profitno_lit_na_Bolgariyu)
- Weight-loss prelander guidance from a CPA network: "before/after" format, amateur photos and videos are the most trusted, plus personal stories and expert opinion — [CPA.live, Jun 2026](https://cpa.live/articles/nutra-vertikal-zolotaya-zhila-ili-lovushka-dlya-novichkov/)
- A scam-copy tell named by Tencheva: text that is an "absurd mix created by a third-rate tabloid hack and edited by AI", recognisable "from kilometres away" — [Plovdiv24, Aug 2026](https://www.plovdiv24.bg/novini/Bylgaria/Otkradnaha-liceto-i-glasa-na-Irina-Tencheva-za-falshiva-reklama-za-otslabvane-3009136)

### Inferences
- **Recommended voice:**
  - Narrator story (testimonial-style advertorial): first person, warm, informal internal monologue ("Бях на 47 и мислех, че просто такава ми е съдбата…")
  - Every direct address to the reader, plus the offer page, form and CTAs: "Вие/Вашият" — "Поръчайте сега", "Ще Ви се обадим"
  - Avoid "ти" for strangers aged 35–60
- Write native Bulgarian, not machine-translated Russian or English. Bulgarians now associate AI-sounding or tabloid copy with scams. Watch for Russianisms, wrong article forms (-ът/-а) and calqued idioms. Use EUR formatting with a comma decimal ("29,90 €").
- Use light, self-deprecating humour about everyday struggles: the "after 40, everything sticks" feeling, cravings at 21:00, menopause belly. Avoid flippant humour about diabetes.
- Length: a long-form story (1,500–2,500 words) works with skimmable subheads. Keep paragraphs to 1–3 lines for mobile, since ~67% of traffic is on phones.
- Use plain metabolic vocabulary: "кръвна захар", "инсулинова резистентност", "глад за сладко", "коремни мазнини", "метаболизъм след 40", "менопауза", "билкова съставка (от растението…)". Avoid jargon such as AMPK unless you explain it in one line.

### Gaps
- No empirical BG A/B data on "Вие" vs "ти" or on copy length was found. These recommendations rest on expert opinion and observed pages.
- I did not collect a corpus of high-performing BG nutra prelander phrases (it would need ad-library scraping).

## Q5. Offer and pricing norms (BGN→EUR transition, bundles, anchors, shipping, guarantees)

### Takeaway
Since 1 Jan 2026 Bulgaria uses the euro (1 EUR = 1.95583 BGN). Mandatory dual BGN/EUR price display ran from 8 Aug 2025 to 8 Aug 2026, and prices are now quoted in EUR, so BGN-only pages look outdated or dodgy. Reference prices:
- Pharmacy berberine capsules: €18–€39 per month
- BG berberine patches: ~€14 per 10 days (unverified)
- CPA weight-loss COD offers: ~€38–39 with a "−50%" crossed-out anchor
- Scam "premium" offers: up to €150 a box
- GLP-1s: €200–475 a month

Free-shipping thresholds sit around €20–45, and "2 for 1" style promos are common.

### Cited Findings
- Euro adoption on 1 Jan 2026, with accounts converted at the fixed rate of **1.95583 BGN per EUR** — [DSK Bank](https://dskbank.bg/za-evroto/individualni-klienti)
- Dual price display was mandatory from **8 Aug 2025 to 8 Aug 2026** (originally planned to the end of 2026, shortened by Parliament in July 2025). Both prices had to be in the same font, size and colour, next to each other, and on receipts too. **After 8 Aug 2026, prices are quoted in euro only.** Price-control rules applied during dual display — [Investor.bg](https://www.investor.bg/a/515-ikonomika-i-makrodanni/417041-kontrolat-varhu-tsenite-pri-dvoyno-oboznachavane-shte-vazhi-do-8-avgust-2026-g); [Economic.bg](https://www.economic.bg/bg/a/view/posledni-dni-s-oboznachavane-na-cenite-v-leva-kakvo-predstoi); [Vuprosi/Novinarnik summaries](https://novinarnik.bg/dokoga-shte-prodalzhi-dvojnoto-oboznachavane-na-cenite-v-levove-i-evro/)
- **Pharmacy berberine benchmarks (EUR, 2026):** Efime 60 caps €18.45; Nixen 500 mg × 90 €23.95; Haya Labs 400 mg × 60 €18.41; Dr. Green "Берберин Слим" 60 caps €24.54; Thorne 200 mg × 60 €35.65; Pure Nutrition complex €38.35 — [Framar and other pharmacy results](https://apteka.framar.bg/30120510/%D0%B5%D1%84%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%B5-%D0%B1%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B1%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BD-%D0%BA%D0%B0%D0%BF%D1%81%D1%83%D0%BB%D0%B8-60). Revita.bg: €19.22–€93.87 across 25 SKUs — [Revita](https://revita.bg/by-category/berberin)
- **Berberine patches in BG:** "Берберинови пачове за тяло" from €13.95 for 10 days, with COD — [cleartoxbg.com search snippet](https://cleartoxbg.com/) (the site did not resolve when I tried to open it, so this is unverified)
- **A typical CPA weight-loss COD page** (Easyloss BG, checked 3 Oct 2026):
  - 75 BGN "now" vs 144 BGN crossed out (~−48%), **still BGN-only** in October 2026
  - "Само днес, на 03.10.26" date-stamp urgency and an "остават 23 пакета" stock counter
  - COD, 5–7 day delivery, "wait for the operator's call"
  - Generic "dietitians and endocrinologists approve"
  - Testimonials with non-Bulgarian placeholder names ("Cory Kirlin", "Mathilde Mayert")
  
  — [Easyloss BG](https://easyloss.shop/bulgaria/)
- **Scam pricing reference:** "Nano Slim" was €150 per box, sold through a call centre — [Plovdiv24, Aug 2026](https://www.plovdiv24.bg/novini/Bylgaria/Otkradnaha-liceto-i-glasa-na-Irina-Tencheva-za-falshiva-reklama-za-otslabvane-3009136)
- **Shipping and promo norms at BG supplement retailers:**
  - Fitness1: free shipping over €43.46 (85 BGN), otherwise €3.58 (7 BGN)
  - Slavento pharmacy: free over €35.79 (69.99 BGN)
  - Vitagold: free over €20.40, with a "2 for the price of 1" promo on a slimming product
  - Pharmalife: €7.49 delivery up to 1 kg, COD
  
  — [search results: Fitness1, Slavento, Vitagold, Pharmalife](https://fitness1.bg/how-to-order/)
- **GLP-1 price ceiling** that consumers are aware of: Mounjaro €204–€475 a month; Wegovy ~€160–225 — see Q2 sources ([MoiteApteki](https://moiteapteki.bg/lekarstvo/mounjaro/); [Diabetico](https://diabetico.bg/cena-na-ozempik-v-bulgaria/))
- **Consumer law:** 14-day withdrawal without giving a reason. If the merchant hasn't said in advance that return costs fall on the consumer, the merchant pays them — [BNR, Dec 2025](https://bnrnews.bg/horizont/post/397516/zadava-li-se-krayat-na-nalozheniya-platezh); [DA Law](https://www.da-law.eu/blog/dlzhite-li-pari-sled-otkazana-onlayn-porchka)
- The CPA view is that the BG audience responds to "discounts and promotions" — [GdeTraffic, 2025](https://gdetraffic.com/Analitika/Bolgariya_obzor_GEO_Kak_profitno_lit_na_Bolgariyu)

### Inferences
- **Price in EUR only**, written the Bulgarian way ("29,90 €" or "€29,90"). Optionally add a small "(≈58,48 лв.)" reference line for older buyers who still think in leva. Don't show BGN-only prices: as of October 2026 that marks a page as stale or non-compliant (see Easyloss).
- **Price positioning:** one-month equivalents sit between pharmacy capsules (€18–39) and GLP-1s (€200+). A sensible COD structure is roughly 1 pack ≈ €29–39, 2 packs ≈ €49–59, 3 packs ≈ €69–79. Show the per-day cost (e.g. "под 1 € на ден") and compare it with "a coffee", not with Ozempic's price in a way that implies equivalence. (Inference; validate against FitPatches' unit economics.)
- **Bundles:** "2+1 подарък" / "Купи 2, вземи 3" is familiar from pharmacy promos. Tie bundle size to a "full 60–90-day course" story, because that also raises the average order value per COD shipment and so dilutes refusal cost. Make the 3-pack the anchored "best value" with free shipping, and charge shipping on 1 pack (≈€3.50–4.50, in line with retailer norms).
- **Anchors:** a "−50%" crossed-out price is standard in BG CPA pages, and therefore also a scam cue. Use an honest anchor that you have actually charged ("Редовна цена" vs "Цена при първа поръчка"). Avoid auto-dated "само днес" and fake stock counters. The 2026 dual-display price-control era made consumers and КЗП sensitive to unfair price presentation.
- **Guarantee:** "30 дни гаранция за връщане на парите" is credible only with a real BG company name and phone next to it. Always state the legal 14-day withdrawal right. A clear, specific guarantee ("Ако не сте доволна, обадете се на +359… и ще Ви възстановим сумата") differentiates you from scam pages that disappear.
- **Gifts:** a low-cost but useful add-on (meal-plan PDF or printed booklet "7-дневно меню за стабилна кръвна захар", a measuring tape) fits the self-care audience. Physical gifts also make refusal feel like losing something (inference).

### Gaps
- No systematic dataset of BG nutra price points in EUR after the changeover (e.g. scraped CPA offer prices) was found.
- The 7-Slim Active "€39 in Bulgaria" price appeared only in a search snippet ([ZorbasMedia](https://zorbasmedia.ru/top5-farm-partnerok-s-nutra-offerami/), page returned 503), so it is unverified.
- Rules on rounding and "psychological prices" after the euro switch, and КЗП enforcement statistics on online price display, were not retrieved.

## Q6. Competitive landscape: who sells berberine and weight-loss patches/supplements in BG, and how they advertise

Berberine in Bulgaria is a crowded **pharmacy capsule** category: 25+ SKUs, mostly imported or white-label, sold on blood sugar, cholesterol and weight management. Berberine **patches** are a small DTC niche, which makes FitPatches' format a real differentiator. Be careful: whether transdermal berberine delivery is effective is a claims-substantiation risk, so make "how it works" claims modest.
- **Typical BG/RO/GR COD prelander template** (from CPA practice; inferred from the sources above):
  1. News or health-blog article with an authority figure
  2. Problem → "discovery" → mechanism
  3. Before/after
  4. Comment wall
  5. "−50% today" + stock counter
  6. 2-field form (name + phone)
  7. Call-centre confirmation and upsell
  
  FitPatches should keep the parts that work (story, mechanism, before/after UGC, short form, call confirmation) and drop the burned parts (fake media, fake authority, fake scarcity, fake comments).

FitPatches' Meta traffic is mostly *problem-aware*, so the advertorial must introduce berberine (e.g. "растителна съставка от корена и кората на кисел трън (Berberis)". - The Meta Ad Library was not scraped for active BG berberine or patch advertisers and their creatives (needs a logged-in or API session).
- Sales, market shares and ad spend of competitors are unknown.

## Q7. Public data on CPA nutra performance in Bulgaria (payouts, approve, buyout)

### Takeaway
Bulgaria-specific approve and buyout rates are not published. Available proxies:
- BG COD nutra payouts of ~$20–35 per approved lead (2023 listings); generic nutra $30–40 in 2025–26
- COD nutra approve rates of 25–50%, with landing form conversion of 3–6%
- CEE COD buyout (all e-commerce) of ~76–85% (Romania 85%)
- Call-centre recovery adds 10–15 percentage points of buyout

### Cited Findings
- BG COD offer payouts: Diet Drops (weight loss) $20.50; GoldRevive (beauty) $35. TerraLeads listings from 2023 — [Affplus](https://www.affplus.com/o/diet-drops-weightloss-cpa-cod-nutra-bg); [Affplus](https://www.affplus.com/o/goldrevive-beauty-cpa-cod-nutra-bg)
- Generic nutra payouts reach "$30–40 per confirmed order" in 2025–26 (search summary, not BG-specific) — [search result set incl. CPA.live/ZorbasMedia](https://cpa.live/articles/nutra-vertikal-zolotaya-zhila-ili-lovushka-dlya-novichkov/)
- COD nutra benchmarks (no BG-specific data): COD payouts $20–60; COD form submission 3–6%; approval 25–50%. The same source gives an "industry average" COD approval of 15–18% (internally inconsistent) — [INB.bio, 10 Dec 2025](https://inb.bio/en/blog/getting-started/nutra-offer-types/)
- COD buyout by country for general e-commerce (2026): Romania 85% (AOV €105), Slovakia 84%, Hungary 82%, Lithuania 76%. Bulgaria, Greece and Croatia data are "on request". Real-time call-centre recovery "swings the buyout rate 10–15%" — [WAPI](https://wapi.com/cash-on-delivery/)
- In the COD model, the affiliate is paid only after the customer actually redeems the parcel from the courier. COD dominates Tier-2/3 GEOs with high refusal shares, including Eastern Europe — [GdeTraffic / Russian CPA search summary](https://gdetraffic.com/Analitika/Bolgariya_obzor_GEO_Kak_profitno_lit_na_Bolgariyu)
- The BEA chair names unclaimed parcels as the "huge problem" of COD for BG merchants — [BNR, Dec 2025](https://bnrnews.bg/horizont/post/397516/zadava-li-se-krayat-na-nalozheniya-platezh)

### Inferences
- For FitPatches' funnel model (inference, not a source), plan against these ranges:
  - Lead → confirmed order: ~50–80% for a direct brand with a clear price on the page. CPA approve rates of 25–50% are low partly because their pages hide the price until the call.
  - Confirmed → redeemed: ~80–90% buyout for a cheap consumable with SMS/Viber reminders. Lockers or offices plus reminders push it higher.
  - Every 5 points of refusal at ~€4–6 two-way shipping per parcel matters at a €30–60 AOV.
- Show the full price, including delivery, *on the page*. CPA's "price revealed on the call" tactic raises approve-stage drop-off and refusals. A transparent page selects for buyers who will redeem.

### Gaps
- No public BG-specific approve or buyout rates from EverAd, Dr.Cash, LeadBit, KMA, M1, AdCombo, Partnerkin or similar were found. Most GEO stats sit behind affiliate logins.
- No BG case studies with ROI figures for weight-loss COD in 2025–26 were found. Results were for other GEOs (CIS, Africa, LatAm).

## Q8. Synthesis: concrete recommendations for FitPatches' advertorial and offer page in Bulgaria

### Takeaway
Win by being the visibly *legitimate, local* option in a market that has just been taught to distrust nutra ads:
- A clearly labelled, story-driven advertorial in respectful "Вие"-Bulgarian
- Real Bulgarian women as proof, plus a real consenting pharmacist or nutritionist
- The blood-sugar / cravings / after-40 angle, with an affordable alternative-to-injections frame
- An EUR price shown up front, a 2–3-pack bundle, COD to office or locker with inspection, SMS/Viber confirmation, and a Bulgarian company with ЕИК and a +359 phone

### Cited Findings
- Scam signatures to avoid, as named by authorities and media: fake TV/news pages, celebrity and doctor deepfakes, miracle claims, fake comments, foreign numbers — [КЗП](https://kzp.bg/bg/potrebiteli/protect/magical-drugs); [Plovdiv24](https://www.plovdiv24.bg/novini/Bylgaria/Otkradnaha-liceto-i-glasa-na-Irina-Tencheva-za-falshiva-reklama-za-otslabvane-3009136); [Nauka.bg](https://nauka.bg/falshivite-lekari-onlain-raboti-izmamata-imitirani-medii-falshivi/)
- COD is ~48% of payments, 70.1% prefer office or locker, ~1 in 4 parcels goes to a locker — [Bloomberg TV BG](https://www.bloombergtv.bg/a/17-v-razvitie/153567-pazarat-na-elektronna-targoviya-u-nas-e-sred-nay-barzo-rastyashtite-v-iztochna-evropa); [Business Новините](https://businessnovinite.bg/bg-biznes/vsjaka-chetvarta-pratka-ot-pokupki-onlajn-se-dostavja-do-avtomat-u-nas.html)
- Women are the most susceptible to health ads; 35% have bought because of ads; a doctor/pharmacist campaign is active — [BTA, Sep 2026](https://www.bta.bg/bg/news/bulgaria/1197265-dva-miliona-balgari-sa-izpolzvali-produkt-reklamiran-kato-polezen-za-zdraveto-)
- EUR-only pricing after 8 Aug 2026 — [Economic.bg](https://www.economic.bg/bg/a/view/posledni-dni-s-oboznachavane-na-cenite-v-leva-kakvo-predstoi)
- EFSA's berberine safety opinion is pending — [NutraIngredients](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2026/07/31/safety-in-question-what-is-the-future-of-berberine/)

### Inferences
**1. Advertorial format**
- Use a "health column / personal story" layout with your own brand masthead. Don't clone any outlet.
- Add a small "Платена публикация" label and an author box with a real name.
- Lead with a relatable BG persona, e.g. "Даниела, 52, учителка от Пловдив" (only if real or clearly labelled as a composite).

**2. Angle hierarchy for women 35–60**
1. Sugar cravings and evening hunger
2. Belly fat after 40 / menopause
3. Insulin resistance (a widely used term in BG; under-diagnosis stats support "може и Вие да не знаете")
4. "Without injections": GLP-1s cost €200+ and are not covered for weight loss

Avoid "cures diabetes" and any medicine-replacement claim.

**3. Proof stack**
- Real customer UGC with first name + initial, age and city, using unpolished photos and short phone videos
- Real review count and star distribution, including a few 3–4★ reviews
- A named Bulgarian pharmacist or nutritionist on video who explains the patch format and contraindications
- Ingredient and dosing panel
- 1–2 study references described cautiously

**4. Explicit anti-scam block**
"Как да разпознаете оригиналния продукт: продава се само от [фирма], ЕИК …, тел. +359 …; не използваме лица на известни личности; плащате при получаване след преглед."

**5. Offer page**
- EUR prices; 1/2/3-pack ladder with the 3-pack anchored as the course; free delivery on 2+ packs
- An honest crossed-out reference price; no auto-dated urgency
- A real, time-bound launch or seasonal promo, if one exists
- Gift: menu booklet
- "30 дни гаранция" plus a statement of the 14-day legal right

**6. Form and logistics**
- Fields: name, phone (with +359 validation) and courier pick-list (Econt office / Speedy office or locker / BOX NOW locker). Address delivery as an option.
- After the form: immediate SMS/Viber with the total, then a confirmation call from a Bulgarian-speaking agent during working hours, then a delivery-day reminder.
- Offer "преглед" on delivery and card-on-delivery.
- Allow a small prepayment discount, but never require it.

**7. Tone**
- "Вие" in every CTA and microcopy line; plain words; short mobile paragraphs
- Avoid Russianisms and AI-sounding prose

**8. Compliance hedge**
- Avoid "безопасно за всички / без странични ефекти"
- List contraindications (pregnancy and breastfeeding, people on antidiabetic or blood-pressure medication, under 18)
- Prepare alternative copy and SKU positioning in case the EU restricts berberine after the EFSA opinion

### Gaps
- None of these recommendations has been validated with BG A/B test data. They are reasoned from market data, regulator and media signals, and competitor observation. FitPatches' own Meta, Shopify and courier data (approve, buyout and refusal by delivery type) should be used to calibrate them.
