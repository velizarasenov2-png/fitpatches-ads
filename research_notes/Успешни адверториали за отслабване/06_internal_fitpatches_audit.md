## Q1. What is in each advertorial? (headline, angle, format, narrator, length, CTAs, proof, offer, product introduction and mechanism)

### Takeaway
Advertorials 01–25 come from one short template of 180–430 words. Each runs: fake news masthead, then a "scientists/doctors are surprised" H1, then a single "new cause" for weight gain, then berberine via AMPK, then the patch beating pills (the "liver destroys 90%" / "3–4x bioavailability" claim), then 1–2 five-star testimonials, then **one** CTA "Провери наличност →". None shows a price. AD26–AD28, the "winners", are 2–5x longer (870–1,015 words). They are built on a voice-of-customer quote and answer objections in order, end in a fake Facebook-style comments section, and also have only one CTA and no price.

### Inferences
- In 01–25 every page is the same 4-beat skeleton. The writer swaps the "new cause" (Metformin, statins, Japan, cortisol, sleep, PCOS, thyroid, cellulite, skin, microbiome, Ozempic…) and keeps the identical mechanism paragraph and CTA box. This is horizontal angle testing at low depth: the pages are too short to build belief before the pitch, roughly 60–120 words of "problem" before the product appears.
- Ten of the 25 angles are disease or drug angles (Metformin, statins/cholesterol, prediabetes, insulin resistance as a disorder, PCOS, thyroid, Ozempic). Several are not even about weight (06 cholesterol, 16 cellulite, 19 skin), which works against a weight-loss offer.

- None of 01–25 or AD26–28 carries any metadata (date launched, ad set, traffic). It is not possible to tell from the files which ones ever received traffic.
- Body copy in the images was not OCR'd. The base64 images (hero pictures, infographics) may contain extra claims or prices that this text extraction did not capture.

## Q2. What do the winners (AD27, AD28, and AD26) do differently?

### Takeaway
The winners give up the "scientists discovered X" news hook. Instead they open with a **verbatim voice-of-customer complaint** from 40+ women ("Ям като врабче и нищо не става", "Пробвала съм всичко"). They clear the reader of blame ("не сте виновни", "проблемът не сте Вие"), build a 3-part hormonal "why diets stop working after 40" explanation and handle objections one after another (skeptics, capsules failed, "what if this fails too"). Only after ~60–70% of the page do they bring in the patch as the delivery fix. They close with social proof in the form of a Facebook-style comment thread. They are 2–5x longer, use the formal "Вие" consistently, target one avatar (women 40+), and are calmer and more hedged. Like the rest, they still have only one CTA and show no price.
