## Q2. Offer structure: pack sizes, bundles, per-unit price, subscriptions, gifts, shipping, guarantees, discount framing, urgency, quizzes, upsells

### Takeaway
Big brands do not compete on single-pack price. Single packs cost **$15–$35**, but every brand pushes buyers into **(a) subscribe-and-save as the default** (15–40% off, free shipping, "skip/pause/cancel anytime"), **(b) volume or free-unit framing** ("Buy 2 Get 1 FREE", "Buy 3 Get 2 FREE", "Order 3 Packs Pay For 2", 3/6/12-month supply tiles), and **(c) multi-product "systems"** (Kind's Metabolic Trio and similar). Direct-response players add 70%-off anchors, timers, a pre-checked shipping-insurance add-on, and one-click post-purchase upsells and downsells at about $10/pack. Every brand advertises a "30-day money-back guarantee", but the fine print is restrictive and drives low Trustpilot scores.

### Inferences
- **Per-pack economics.** FitPatches' €14.99 single pack already sits at Kind's EU price (€16.00) and below every US DR funnel's single-pack price ($34.99). The gap is in the **upper tiers and continuity**. FitPatches' best tier (3+1 = €10.00/pack, 4 packs) is about the same per-pack price as Gentle's post-purchase upsell ($9.99/pack). Gentle, LifeGauge and PatchAid all go up to **9–12 packs**, and their biggest tier is shown first or flagged "Best Deal".
- **Free units beat percentages in headlines.** Kind's main ad and cart mechanic is "Buy 2 Get 1 Free / Order 3 Pay For 2". Gentle's PDP uses "Buy 2 + Get 1 FREE / Buy 3 + Get 2 FREE" with "You save $X" lines. Percentages ("70% OFF") are reserved for the DR advertorial. FitPatches' "3+1 gift" is aligned. A "2+1" middle tier labeled MOST POPULAR (instead of plain "2 packs") would match the leaders.
- **Subscription is the core LTV lever for every big brand** (Kind 15%, TPB 20%, PatchAid 40%, Gentle up to 70–75%). FitPatches' COD model has no continuity. A COD-compatible equivalent would be a "90-day course" framing plus a reorder offer by SMS or phone at day 25.
- **Funnel splitting.** Gentle runs ad traffic to a separate advertorial and one-page checkout rather than the Shopify PDP. It also runs a post-purchase upsell ($9.99/pack), a downsell (1+1 $15.99) and a pre-checked $5.99 add-on. These are classic AOV levers that FitPatches' COD form could mimic legally with opt-in, non-pre-checked add-ons.
- **Guarantee language matters for trust and complaints.** Leaders say "30-day money-back guarantee" but restrict it heavily, and Trustpilot complaints cluster on refunds and subscriptions (see Q4). German dropship competitors advertise 60–180 days.

### Gaps
- Kind UK (GBP) prices could not be fetched (en-gb localized JSON not available; kindpatches.co.uk returned 403).
- Gentle's actual post-purchase upsell page was not reached (it would require placing an order). The upsell prices come from catalog products named "cc-upsell" and "cc-downsell", which strongly implies their use but is not observed directly.
- Purisaki's and LifeWave's product prices were not captured.

## Q3. PDP / landing layout: above the fold on mobile, sticky add-to-cart, section order

### Takeaway
Two distinct archetypes emerged. **Brand-led** pages (Kind, The Patch Brand, PatchAid) have a calm product-card fold with no timers, no stars above the fold (Kind), and a subscription toggle as the first purchase element. **Direct-response** pages (Gentle's PDP and advertorial) put stars, a benefit headline, 3 check-bullets and a **stacked bundle-card selector** high on the page, followed by 15 payment icons, guarantee accordions and a long-form persuasion sequence (benefits → ingredients → timeline → before/after → how-to → reviews → FAQ). FitPatches' current pattern (strikethrough -50% plus countdown) belongs to the DR archetype.

### Inferences (for the report writer, to translate into FitPatches recommendations)
1. **Offer ladder:** consider restructuring as 1 pack (€14.99) / **2+1 "Най-избиран"** ("most chosen", ~€27.99–29.99, ≈€9.33–10/pack) / **3+2 or 4+2 "Най-изгодно"** ("best value", ≈€8–9/pack). Preselect the middle tier and show "Спестяваш €X" ("You save €X") per tier, as Gentle and Kind do. Today FitPatches' 2-pack (€14.00/pack) gives almost no incentive over 1 pack.
2. **Course framing:** label tiers by duration ("1 месец / 2 месеца + 1 подарък / Курс 3 месеца"; "1 month / 2 months + 1 gift / 3-month course") with a timeline module ("седмица 1–2 / месец 1 / месец 2–3", i.e. "weeks 1–2 / month 1 / months 2–3") using soft, non-numeric language. This mirrors Gentle's 1+/2+/3+ months and PatchAid's 3/6/12-month supply.
3. **COD-compatible continuity:** big brands' LTV comes from subscriptions. For COD, a day-25 SMS or phone reorder offer, or a "subscribe by phone, pay each delivery" option, is the analogue. Making "Без абонамент, без карта" ("No subscription, no card") a visible trust badge is a counter-position to the leaders' Trustpilot complaints.
4. **Cross-sell system:** Kind's growth engine is stacks (Trio $25.50 subscription vs $45). FitPatches is single-SKU. A second SKU (sleep or "unpuff"/bloat) bundled as a "system" is the main AOV lever the leaders use.
5. **Urgency calibration:** Kind (the largest) uses no timers. Gentle uses heavy timers and 70% anchors but suffers on trust. FitPatches' permanent "-50%" plus countdown is closer to the DR end; keeping a single believable sale reason (seasonal or launch) may outperform a perpetual timer.
