# How big DTC supplement & weight-management brands structure offers and PDP/LP design (teardowns observed 2 Oct 2026)

Method note: unless a different date is given, every observation below comes from a live page captured on **2 Oct 2026**. Captures used Playwright with a mobile iPhone 13 profile (390 px wide), plus the Shopify `/products.json` and `/products/<handle>.js` endpoints for exact prices. All prices are in **USD**. Screenshots, contact sheets and the visible text of each page are saved in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-fitpatches-ads/77d5f012-1d96-5267-aa86-a2c530bc0277/scratchpad/shots/` (files `supp-*-fold.png`, `supp-*-full.png`, `supp-*.txt`, `sheets/supp-*-VP.png` = first 4 mobile screens side by side).
Brands analysed: Lemme (Reset, formerly "GLP-1 Daily"), Pendulum (GLP-1 Probiotic), Arrae (MB-1), Supergut (GLP-1 Daily Support), GOLO (Release, which contains berberine-type metabolic ingredients and is aimed at insulin resistance), Glucose Goddess / "Glucose Revolution" (Anti-Spike), Ritual, Seed (DS-01) and AG1 (secondary sources only, because the site blocks headless browsers). Kind Patches (Berberine Patches) is included because it is a direct berberine-patch competitor. Codeage, Toniiq and Goli are covered briefly.

## Q0. Which brands are the category leaders, and how big are they?

### Takeaway
The brands that set the standard are $100M+ businesses (AG1, Arrae, Lemme, GOLO). The one that matters most for FitPatches is **Kind Patches**. It sells the same product as FitPatches (30 berberine patches, "GLP-1 Friendly") at almost the same price ($15 vs €14.99), and its estimated web revenue is in the eight figures per month.

### Inferences
- The US "GLP-1 support / berberine" market is crowded and mature (Schwartz stage 4–5). Leaders compete on clinically named ingredients, mechanism stories and subscription economics, not on discount size.
- Kind Patches is the closest benchmark for FitPatches on format and price. Lemme, Arrae and GOLO are the benchmarks for copy and structure aimed at women.

### Gaps
- The Ad Library API returned no destination URLs, so I could not confirm which exact landing page each brand sends Meta traffic to. GOLO's ad-style pages (`/pages/release-pdp-lp`, `/pages/release-listicle`) were found through search and reviewed. AG1's modular `/landers/...` URLs were found through search but could not be loaded.
- No reliable revenue figures for Pendulum, Supergut, Seed, Ritual, Glucose Revolution or Kind Patches; the Kind figure is an estimate only.

## Q1. Brand-by-brand teardowns (mobile PDP / LP)

### Takeaway
Every leader shows a strikethrough price, but the "original" price is its own real one-time price, and the discount is modest (12–35%). Nearly all of them pre-select a subscription and often a multi-month plan. They back claims with named, patented ingredients and study numbers, and they use calm, premium visuals. None uses a countdown timer, and none names Ozempic on the PDP.

## Q7. Pattern summary: what the high-converting leaders do differently from FitPatches

### Takeaway
Compared with FitPatches (−50% strikethroughs, a countdown timer, "vs Ozempic", one doctor), the leaders use real reference prices with modest, clearly explained savings. They pre-select the best-value multi-unit plan and tie a gift to it, and they state a guarantee with a length right under the CTA. They create urgency through dated promos, not timers, and compare against formats or other supplements rather than a branded drug. They layer proof (counts, experts, ingredient studies, age-matched UGC) and keep the visual design calm and premium.

### Cited Findings
